BULLETS OF FURY - OFFICIAL TESTER BUILD - NOVEMBER 5, 2026

START THE GAME (WINDOWS 10 / 11)
1. Extract the ENTIRE ZIP into a normal folder. Do not run inside the ZIP.
2. Open that folder and double-click START-BOF.cmd.
3. Your browser opens the game. Click inside it once to enable sound.
4. Keep the server window open while playing. Press Enter there to stop it.

Everything the game needs is included: code, images, animation banks, music,
sound effects, fonts and data. Windows PowerShell and your existing desktop
browser run the build; Python, Node, a separate installer and internet access
are not required for the game. Edge or Chrome is recommended for this test.
Opening index.html directly from disk will not load all game resources.
The launcher normally uses the same local address so saves survive restarts.
Keep using the same browser/profile and avoid private browsing for saves.

MOUSE AND KEYBOARD
Move: W A S D or arrow keys.
Fire primary: hold LEFT mouse button, or J.
Retina targeting: RIGHT mouse button, or C. Click to acquire/cycle a target.
Missiles: SPACE, or K. With a Retina lock, missiles home onto that target.
Multi-Retina: hold MOUSE BUTTON 4 (the first/Back thumb button), or hold Y.
Three-button mouse: tap RIGHT click, release, then quickly RIGHT click again
and HOLD the second press to enter Multi-Retina. Keep holding while it scans.
Press SPACE to launch the marked-target missile volley.
Charge: H or mouse button 5 (the second/Forward thumb button).
Barrel roll: double-tap left or right. Somersault: double-tap up.
Pause: Enter or P. Menus: arrows/WASD, Enter to confirm, Esc/K to go back.
Backspace edits text; it is not menu Back.

Single right-click does not fire missiles. Releasing the Multi-Retina hold
stops acquiring new targets; existing marks retain the game's normal timer.
The right-click menu and thumb-button browser navigation are blocked on the
playfield. Options can remap controls. Older saved mouse/Space controls are
migrated once; unrelated keyboard and controller choices are preserved.

TEST BUILD NOTES
This snapshot includes the latest Razorwake zigzags, Thunderhead reactor,
Sumpjaw tandem changes and all current runtime assets. Normal's campaign ends
after Stage 5; Hard and Furious continue. It starts with normal player damage,
not the invulnerable bot used in review videos.

The older full regression harness has a known missing-helper setup error;
this is not a claim that every test or full campaign has passed. Focused
mouse/keyboard and clean-extracted-package checks accompany this build.
Please report stage, pilot, difficulty, weapon and what happened for any bug.

NOVEMBER 6 TESTER REPAIRS
After training, Enter/Start or the Fire/Confirm button deploys the highlighted
campaign mission. Back/Esc opens the map menu; Save and Load remain separate
choices. Mouse presses are buffered so quick clicks survive a slow frame.

Flamethrower: hold primary fire and close the distance. Sweep its plume across
nearby enemies to damage several together and leave them burning. It has a
shorter reach than the machine gun/laser. Levels 1-5 increase reach, width,
damage and burn time. This build adds 27 pixels of visible reach at every level;
fire damage now keeps pace with machine-gun upgrades. Sweep formations to
make the most of it. Fire-resistant enemies still call for another weapon.

Short sound effects now use a bounded decoded cache and the shared mixer.
Music and held weapon loops retain their streaming playback. Space ship art
loads/decodes in a bounded queue; all high-quality frames are retained.

# MAGMA COLOSSUS — FULL SPEC (Mike, drop 0801iu)

Recorded close to verbatim. Where I am unsure I have marked it **OPEN** rather than
guessing.

> "this boss is to demonstrate that I know what to do with anchor points"

---

## 0. THE RIG

**Head, torso, arms, legs all remain SEPARATE PIECES.** Nothing is baked. Every
piece is anchored and driven independently.

The cannons hang off the arms **on chains, socket to socket**.

---

## 1. THE CORE ANIMATION (torso only)

The middle yellow light — the fusion core.

1. It **lights up from bottom to top**
2. Then the whole core **spreads a bright orange/white flash left and right**, like
   a split outward from that middle line

This is the torso's own animation and belongs to it alone.

---

## 2. THE CINEMATIC INTRO

Full sequence, in order:

1. Player approaches the end of the level
2. **Out of nowhere the TORSO comes out from the side where the lava is**
3. It **slowly levitates and floats to the top/middle of the screen** — fully
   visible, **static, no animation yet**
4. The **core lights in the middle and begins powering on**. Sound carries this.
5. As the core powers on, a **chain drops from the chain hook on the LEFT shoulder,
   behind the torso**
   - mechanical **"werrrrr"** sound
   - **screen shakes**
   - the chain **thrusts into the lava to the left at an angle**, medium/fast
   - **retracts the LEFT ARM** in close to the torso and **anchors it**
   - both pieces now levitating
6. The **RIGHT shoulder** does the same — hook, grab the right arm, retract, anchor,
   levitate
7. The hooks then take **the legs, one by one**, retract both
8. **Simulate motion with all anchored pieces**
9. The **cannons come up with the arms** but **dangle on chains, socket to socket**
10. The chains finally **send off to the top of the screen** and retract **the
    HEAD** — it comes down at **medium speed**, then **slows as it nears the
    socket**
11. On connection: a sound to **finalise the transformation**
12. The **eyes "turn on"** via pixel flash/glow
13. The **whole body** does the bottom-to-top orange/white flash
14. Then: **arms and cannons lift** as if raising them, **head tilts up**,
    **EVERYTHING rigorously shakes**, and a sound simulates **a mech screaming**
15. **Then the fight begins.**

---

## 3. THE FIGHT — DAMAGE ORDER

**The head cannot be attacked until everything else is destroyed.**

| stage | what is vulnerable |
|---|---|
| 1 | **cannons, legs, arms** — these first |
| | torso **flashes white/orange with the core** as a 16-bit **energy shield** |
| 2 | once the limbs are gone, **the torso only** |
| 3 | when the torso dies, the **head floats free** |

**Head phase:** shoots **beams from its eyes like a cyclops** — use the player laser
beams, colour-swapped to **orange/white**.

---

## 4. ATTACKS

**The core** shoots energy projectiles at the player throughout.

**The cannons**, being anchored pieces, can turn. They do:
- spread
- machine-gun style
- rapid fire
- charge-up fire blasts

**The core charge** — a giant fireball.
- use the **green helix ball at half charge**, palette-swapped to **orange/white**
- **one frame only**
- make it **glow**
- **shoots off fast**, and either **bursts on impact** or **bursts halfway across
  the screen**

# THE CONFIRMED ORDER (Mike, drop 0801jo)

Supersedes the earlier sequence. This is the running order.

1. **Torso appears**
2. **Grabs the LEFT arm/shoulder**, retracts
3. **Grabs the RIGHT arm**, retracts
4. **A chain SPINE drops from the bottom hole**
5. The chains **spread out and grab the legs, one by one**
6. **Retract one by one**
7. An **iris forms in the MIDDLE of the top circle and spreads**
8. **The head rises**
9. **White flash** over the whole thing
10. **Lifts the arms and cannons**
11. **Mechanical scream** — shakes the whole screen
12. Returns to his **new idle**: glowing torso, glowing core, and the **magma on his
    shoulders lighting up along its pixel lines**

Note the change at step 4: it is a chain SPINE from the bottom hole that then
branches to the legs - not two separate runs.

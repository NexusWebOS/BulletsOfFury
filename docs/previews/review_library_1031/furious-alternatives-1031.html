What stays consistent

Fair pressure

One heavy attack at a time. Full tell, commitment, active move and recovery. Broken weapons cancel their own warnings and shots. Never solve Furious by shrinking every dodge gap.

Readable weapons

Ordinary bullets and energy remain unshootable. Physical missiles keep their interception rule. The hammer keeps its missile-only reversal. No added bouncing glass or hidden emitters.

Earned damage windows

Background forms stay protected, foreground forms stay hittable. Preserve current HP pools, limbs, healthy-copy returns and final death. No extra life bars or automatic refills proposed.

# Campaign playtest: Stages 1–6, Normal / Hard / Furious

September 26, 2026. This is an observational playtest and weekend work list, not a gameplay change.

## What was actually exercised

I opened the shipping `index.html` in real Chromium, selected Campaign, Axel, and each difficulty through the game UI, and controlled movement, primary fire, and missiles with keyboard input. I ran stages 1–6 in order on Normal, then Hard, then Furious. Stages 2–6 were entered as separate campaign-mode segments rather than earned by clearing the preceding stage. Each stage clock and encounter gate then ran naturally: no miniboss or boss was force-spawned in these 18 primary recordings. The controller's first life was vulnerable; after its first death it became an invulnerable observer so late fights could be inspected. The pilot has crude aiming and does not roll, somersault, or optimize upgrades. Its first deaths were usually within the first few seconds. **These recordings do not establish human clear rates, dodge fairness, or a difficulty ranking.** They do establish visual pacing, encounter order, attack selection, and some spawn positions. The browser was muted, so audio was not assessed.

Recordings and event logs live under [`_shots/campaign_playtest_0926/`](../_shots/campaign_playtest_0926/). Each difficulty has `stage1_*.mp4` through `stage6_*.mp4`, matching `stageN.json`, and screenshots. MP4s are accelerated visual reviews: one sampled game second per frame, encoded at four frames per second. All 18 main runs, 10 direct-boss supplements, and seven cutscene captures reported zero page/console errors. The direct-boss clips skip the field and miniboss gate and use invulnerability, so they are labelled separately. Campaign intro and after-stage scenes were also entered through their authored state machines and recorded; these are isolated scene inspections, not natural clears.

## Findings, ranked for the weekend

1. **Stage 3 opening and boss variety — highest priority.** At four seconds into the actual Furious stage recording, Axel's large conversation panel occupies the lower playfield while ice jets are already attacking ([frame](../_shots/campaign_playtest_0926/furious/stage3_furious_004s.png)). Let the stage intro resolve before lethal waves, or keep the radio message in a small HUD panel during combat. On Normal and Hard, the 90-second Rime Wall samples repeatedly choose `s3wallcannons` and `s3wallhunt`; Normal only reached `s3wallgate` late, and Hard showed no gate/halo/overdrive at all. The early override in `stage3BossAttack` selects hunt on alternating steps below phase 3 (`assets/game.js`, around line 16528), while the cannon sequence consumes a long stretch. Move gate/halo into the first two or three attack cycles, shorten the repeated opening warning, and add an aimed, clearly telegraphed anti-camp variation. Preserve a learnable safe path. Re-test with controlled weapons and human dodging before increasing HP or projectile speed. [Normal Rime Wall clip](../_shots/campaign_playtest_0926/normal/boss_focus/stage3_normal_boss_focus.mp4), [Hard clip](../_shots/campaign_playtest_0926/hard/boss_focus/stage3_hard_boss_focus.mp4), [Stage 2 comparison](../_shots/campaign_playtest_0926/normal/boss_focus/stage2_normal_boss_focus.mp4).

2. **Fix actual side-entry pop-in; separate it from bay deployments.** The Stage 2 Hard event log contains a `skim` born roughly three pixels inside the right camera edge after a delayed wave command. The position was calculated before `aiDelay`; camera movement made it stale at spawn time (`assets/game.js`, near the `offRightX(44)` wave descriptor and `encounterRipple`). Compute offscreen entry from the camera on the actual spawn frame and include the full sprite half-width plus margin. Furious Stage 2 ash drops and Furious Stage 6 Hivewing launches also begin in view, but they originate at visible carrier bays and have launch effects. Those should be judged on the clarity of their deployment cue, not counted as the same defect. The logical spawn audit did **not** prove asset-loading visual pop-in; that still needs slow-load video. [Stage 2 Hard log](../_shots/campaign_playtest_0926/hard/stage2.json), [Stage 6 Furious clip](../_shots/campaign_playtest_0926/furious/stage6_furious.mp4).

3. **Make shield and module objectives legible, then inspect later phases.** In the Stage 4 boss sample the Storm Sovereign remains shielded while the observer fires into it; its hull bar stays full even as damage accounting rises. This is consistent with hits on the shield/nodes and poor aiming, not proof of a broken fight. Add a short readable module cue and stronger visual feedback on correct and incorrect impact points, then run a skilled/manual break-through test on all three difficulties. Warhive similarly repeats descend/open/launch/hold/retreat/away because the observer did not focus its two thrusters. Its ace and late attacks need a separate targeted playtest. [Stage 4 frame](../_shots/campaign_playtest_0926/furious/stage4_boss_late.png), [Warhive focus](../_shots/campaign_playtest_0926/furious/boss_focus/stage6_furious_boss_focus.mp4).

4. **Tighten Stage 6 carrier pacing without erasing its escort mechanic.** In the controlled Furious Warhive sample, the carrier retreated around 15 seconds and did not return until about 60 seconds because surviving escorts kept the bay cycle open; code also allows an away timeout of 45 seconds. The escort fight fills much of that interval, but the boss itself is gone. Consider an earlier return pressure cue, a shorter upper cap, and a bounded number of escorts alive at once. Preserve the carrier's memorable bay reveal and the need to defeat its modules. Test both Stage 6 route choices separately; this pass did not do that.

5. **Difficulty should add decisions, not only multiply values.** The current core scalar set increases enemy bullet speed, fire rate, health, and density from Normal to Hard to Furious (`assets/game.js`, around line 7896). Stage 1 field counts in these captures were similar across difficulties, so most of its felt escalation comes from attack tempo and patterns. Use Stage 1's zone and sweep tells and Stage 2's early pattern rotation as the reference. For each later boss/miniboss, aim for an additional positioning problem or tighter but fair tell on Hard, then an intentional combo/remix on Furious. Check that a no-roll/no-somersault route still exists for ordinary attacks, while special attacks clearly teach their required counter. Do not infer fair damage from these invulnerable clips.

6. **Polish transitions and visual hierarchy.** The seven authored cinematic state machines all completed without script errors. The Stage 4 reveal visibly uses the approved two-eyed symbiote and the Fury pilot lineup ([frame](../_shots/campaign_playtest_0926/cutscenes/post_stage4_012s.png)); the Stage 6 sewer descent reads as an actual entrance ([frame](../_shots/campaign_playtest_0926/cutscenes/post_stage6_024s.png)). The Stage 3 gameplay dialogue, boss warning text, shield bars, and busy background sometimes compete for the same visual space. Check each boss entrance at game speed, make primary danger tells win the contrast hierarchy, and avoid long dialogue over active hazards. [Intro/aftermath videos](../_shots/campaign_playtest_0926/cutscenes/).

## Encounter notes by stage

- **1:** Normal boss-focus sample and Furious natural run both show the helicopter's major authored phases. The Furious natural boss alone yielded sweep, missile lock, sonic, zone rush, charge tell, and re-entry. The Razorback miniboss remained active a long time under weak tracking; this is not a validated player clear-time complaint.
- **2:** Furnace Reaver is the strongest early reference for fast pattern rotation. Normal, Hard, and Furious direct-boss samples all begin spin → cannon → sweep rather than sitting on one action. Furious's shield survived the 90-second simple-fire sample; a shield-targeting test is needed before calling it too durable.
- **3:** Normal and Hard Rime Wall have a genuine attack-selection repetition in controlled samples. Furious replaces both mini and boss after a scripted strike: Frostcruiser → Thermocloud and Rime Wall → Therno, so it should be evaluated as a separate encounter. Its ice/fire/thermal modes cycle rapidly in the 90-second focus clip. [Furious Therno frame](../_shots/campaign_playtest_0926/furious/stage3_therno_late.png).
- **4:** The Sovereign looks large and threatening, but this pass did not establish that players understand how to open its shield or see its later attacks. Manually target the nodes and measure time to first vulnerability. The bot does not answer that question.
- **5:** Hammer's ball/reversion/strike sequence already gives the fight a stronger identity than the repeated Rime Wall opening. Keep it as a progression target and test its missile knockback/reflected bullet/roll or somersault counters with a human pilot.
- **6:** Tempest Brothers appeared in all three main runs. Warhive was reached in Normal and Furious; Hard capture ended before it. The Warhive mini-fleet launches in view from the carrier by design. The simple center-aim controller never took down the thrusters, so ace behavior remains unverified here.

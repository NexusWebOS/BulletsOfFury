# Interactive cinematic level approaches

Nine full-size environment-only establishing plates connect the campaign map and story scenes to every playable route: eight campaign stages and the Stage 9 bonus route.

## Runtime contract

- Every master is **1672 × 941 RGB PNG**.
- No ships, aircraft, spacecraft, vehicles, pilots, people, enemies, bosses, creatures, projectiles, explosions, dialogue, UI, text or logos are baked into the masters.
- Foregrounds, central routes and selected sky/deck regions are intentionally open for runtime overlays.
- Add actors, vehicles, enemies, interactive props, markers, effects and dialogue in the game layer.
- `interaction_zones.json` provides optional common placement rectangles in native pixel coordinates.

## Routes

1. **Rumble in the Jungle** — tropical coast, river causeway and distant dam.
2. **It's Hot in Here** — pale desert apron descending into the volcanic caldera.
3. **Ice Still Can't See** — abandoned polar terrace and glacial-canyon entrance.
4. **Crouching Missiles, Hidden Death** — empty coastal highway leading to the open airbase.
5. **All for One, None for All** — silent orbital threshold facing Earth and the asteroid route.
6. **Heavy Turbulence** — weather platform and unobstructed day-to-night storm corridor.
7. **Not Another Sewer Level** — dry maintenance platform overlooking the toxic intake.
8. **Furious Death** — empty dais facing the necrotic deep-space frontier.
9. **The Velocity Void** — deserted bonus platform and nine-ring velocity corridor.

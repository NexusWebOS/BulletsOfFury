# Generation Prompt Record

## Shared contract

Built with the built-in ImageGen reference workflow. Each approved perspective level-approach plate was used only for its stage identity, palette and materials; every result is a new overhead layout.

- Exact output: 1672x941 landscape RGB PNG
- True bird's-eye camera, 85-90 degrees straight down, near-orthographic, no horizon
- Lavish Neo-Geo / 1990s arcade military-science-fiction environmental art
- Readable bottom-center entry, open central traversal lane and top-center exit
- Quiet side pockets for optional runtime interactions
- Environment only: no ships, aircraft, vehicles, pilots, people, enemies, bosses, creatures, projectiles, explosions, text, logos, banners, HUD or UI

## Stage direction

1. **Rumble in the Jungle:** turquoise jungle river and islands, stone causeway, ancient-industrial dam threshold.
2. **It's Hot in Here:** armored desert road, tan rock, lava fissures, industrial side platforms and volcanic destination.
3. **Ice Still Can't See:** fractured ice shelf, research-zone remnants, clear ice path and frozen cavern entrance.
4. **Crouching Missiles, Hidden Death:** divided coastal highway, seawalls, ruined suburb blocks, checkpoint apron and empty airbase threshold.
5. **All for One, None for All:** orbital station deck, magnetic launch causeway, circular transit gate, Earth surface and natural asteroids in peripheral gaps.
6. **Heavy Turbulence:** empty weather deck opening into a daylight-to-indigo supercell cloud route with corner pylons.
7. **Not Another Sewer Level:** dry maintenance entry, glowing olive sludge canal, central causeway, pipes, catwalks and dark intake.
8. **Furious Death:** ancient black-metal platform, fractured obsidian path, crimson-violet geology, side terraces and gravitational abyss.
9. **The Velocity Void:** violet-cyan energy-grid platform with dormant geometric gate architecture and a large final concentric gate.

Stage 5 was regenerated after the initial draft to enforce the strict overhead camera. Stage 8 was mechanically normalized by one horizontal pixel to the exact 1672x941 delivery size without changing its composition.

BULLETS OF FURY // PROJECTILE GALLERY

Every distinct projectile the game fires, filmed through the real renderer while the real updatePlay() moves, spins and ages it: all nine stages' regular waves, every miniboss and boss, the Stage 6 rebels, every pilot × weapon × level, charge shots, space weapons and missiles. 24 frames at 30 fps, centred on the round. Click any card for the frame strip and its draw diagnostics.

Backdrops use screen blending, so additive light reads roughly as it does in game. "Soft blur glow" is a shadowBlur smear (the opposite of the 1015 pixel-glow rule, and the costliest per-draw canvas op). "Still" means fewer than 3 distinct frames out of 24. "Procedural" means the round is drawn only from fillRect/arc paths, with no authored art.

What the "after" pass (assets/projectile_fx_1103.js) adds
  • Blur → pixel glow. A requested shadowBlur never reaches the canvas while rounds draw. The round's own silhouette, tinted to the glow colour, is grown in whole pixels as 2–3 hard rings on the PXG brightness ladder and palette-stepped on the game clock.
  • Inner light. Glowing rounds get their own brightest pixels added back on a travelling ladder step, so the core shimmers inside the art.
  • Snake trails. Energy rounds (orbs, plasma, photon, void, lightning, fire, ice…) leave a tail that wriggles across the flight path with a braided counter-strand. Tracers get a two-step streak, and missiles shed flickering ember pixels. Trail colour is read from the round's own art.
  • Spawn pop. Every enemy round is born with a 2-frame pixel ring, so a new shot reads in a dense pattern.
  • Threat rim. Enemy rounds get a 1-px dark outline from their own silhouette, so they stay readable over lava, explosions and cloud.
  • Graze flash. An enemy round passing within a hair of the pilot flashes white-hot and throws sparks. The filmed observer pilot weaves through fire, so some enemy clips show this.
Renderer only: positions, speeds, hitboxes, damage, clocks and RNG are untouched (verified frame-by-frame by _BUILD_SOURCE/probe_projectile_fx_1103.py). Switches: PFX13.cfg.{glow,inner,trails,pop,rim,graze}; window.PFX13_OFF=1 before load disables it all.

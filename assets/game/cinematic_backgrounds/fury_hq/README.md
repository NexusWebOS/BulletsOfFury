# FURY HQ cinematic backgrounds

Nine standalone, character-free cinematic plates for compositing the full-resolution cast poses. Every master is preserved at its native generated size of **1672 × 941**. The contact sheet is the only resized derivative.

## Scene set

- Launch bay and runway
- Main command deck with nine stations
- Tactical briefing classroom with a left-side podium for Cole
- Strategic war room
- Armory and gear-up bay
- Medical and decontamination wing
- Engineering and reactor control
- Squad ready room and mess
- Observation deck over the runway and storm horizon

## Prompt set

Built-in ImageGen was used in reference-image mode. The supplied FURY intro art established the gunmetal military architecture, navy shadows, cyan displays, restrained green insignia, amber practical lights, and crisp late-1990s arcade rendering. Each scene prompt requested a native-size 16:9 opaque environment plate, a clear lower staging plane, side-loaded props, deep perspective, no people, no UI frame, no dialogue box, no readable text, and no 640 × 480 crop.

## Composition notes

- The command deck and war room support full-team blocking.
- The classroom reserves a readable instructor zone behind and beside the podium.
- The bay, armory, medical wing, reactor, and observation deck keep the center aisle clear.
- Masters are opaque RGB backgrounds; composite the RGBA character poses above them without resizing the masters first.

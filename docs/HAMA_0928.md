| breakdowns (1:41.6–1:55, 2:25–2:38) | *BREAK IT DOWN!* — shield up, the troupe regroups and alternates **lasso bars** (arm circling overhead, on the beat) with **jumping 360 turn bars** (front → side → back → side, one view per beat, in the air), chanting **OH! / OH-OH! / OH-OH-OH!** on every beat | free (boss and robots immune) |

**The robot toss.** He throws the hammer up and back over his shoulder ("HOLD MY HAMMER"), grabs the
nearest robot, holds it up while a shared green/yellow/red lane warns (tracks you for the first 60%,
then commits), and chucks it. The robot sings *CAN'T TOUCH THIIIIS!* the whole way in, tumbling. It is a
real target — shoot it down (it pops with *OH NO!*) or it hits you. Then the hammer comes down and he
catches it. If fewer than two robots are left he calls them back first ("ROBOTS! BACK TO THE FLOOR").

**Hammer jumps** are sung: *AHHH-WOOO!* on the way up, *HEY!* from the robots when he lands.

## Lyrics

The captions use the song's short hooks — CAN'T TOUCH THIS, STOP, HAMMER TIME, BREAK IT DOWN, the
OH-OH chant — plus original parody lines for the verses (*MY HAMMER IS CHROME*, *CHROME PARACHUTE
PANTS*, *TOO SHINY FOR YOUR LASERS* …). The song's verses are **not** transcribed. The dialogue face
has no usable apostrophe at caption size, so the hook is lettered CANT.

⚠ **The instrumental is a commercial recording** (and it samples "Super Freak"). It is fine in Mike's
build; a public or Steam release needs a sync licence, or the track swapped. Removing the file only
silences HAMA — HAMMER and the campaign are unaffected.

## How it is built

`hammer_time_0927.js` became track-agnostic (`ht27Variant`): the song, its intro length and its lock
windows come from the variant, and `null` is exactly the old HAMMER. `assets/hama_0928.js` is the
variant plus the new beats, layered on the proven lock, shield, helpers, pause and cleanup paths.

Traps found on the way:

* **The toss re-launched the hammer forever.** The catch cleared `flyHammer`, and the release branch
  keyed on "no hammer in the air", so it threw it again the next frame. Invisible to both pinned-clock
  runs (they re-seeded the mode after the first toss); found by a 46-second real-time run. One-shot
  flag now, and a suite assertion that fails on the old line.
* **`msgText` draws on every path but returns a width on only one** — a caller that falls back when it
  returns nothing draws the line twice, which read as stray bracket glyphs at both ends.
* **`stageText`'s 9th argument is letter spacing**, not a scale — passing `1` spread every letter a full
  glyph apart and pushed BREAK IT DOWN! off both edges.
* **The lock raises the shield wall**; drawn over the STOP, it hid both slams. The immunity stays, the
  wall is not drawn during a STOP.

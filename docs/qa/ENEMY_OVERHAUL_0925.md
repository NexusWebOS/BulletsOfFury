The ground emplacement planner adds 2 waves on Normal, 4 on Hard and 7 on Furious, with none on Easy. The units share a modular base and independently rotated MG, missile, laser or sonic head, and release from an anchored muzzle after a visible tell. Their deaths use the shared ground explosion path. Stage 1 is gated beyond the water approach; the other stages use their biome-specific base and a visible platform fallback where no drivable mask exists. Stages 5 and 9 are intentionally excluded.

Real Chromium visual checks inspected the jungle tanks, Stage 4 jet mounts, Stage 7 toxic tank/skimmer/shield, four ground turret heads, all eight campaign stage fields and bosses, and both Stage 9 encounters. The eight-stage review video and individual screenshots are in ignored `_shots/`. Video segments use developer encounter jumps; they are not full clear recordings.

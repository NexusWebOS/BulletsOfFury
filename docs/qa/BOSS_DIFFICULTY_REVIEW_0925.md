# Boss and miniboss review — 2026-09-25

This review uses the real Chromium renderer and the game encounter code. `probe_boss_difficulty_0925.py` sampled 45 fights: each assigned campaign miniboss and boss on Normal, Hard and Furious, except Stage 8, which intentionally has no miniboss. It left the player invulnerable and did not fire; each miniboss ran for 9 seconds and each boss for 12 seconds. `results.json` and Furious screenshots are under `_shots/boss_difficulty_0925/`. The samples verify opening attacks and art, not full health-gated phases, dodging fairness or complete clears. Bullet counts exclude sustained beam and hazard damage, so they are a pacing signal rather than a difficulty score. No page or console errors were recorded.

The extended Warden probe (`_shots/warden_extended_0925/`) sampled 40 seconds on Normal. After its portal entrance, it alternated leap/leap-back, patrol, rail and shell modes. It launched 13 authored shells and 10 rail rounds by second 40, plus 19 generic `emissile` shots; no browser errors occurred. The large black circle under the walker is the existing opaque portal seal, retained through the fight by design. This sample used invulnerability and did not damage the boss, so it does not validate low-HP transitions.

A later damage-gate probe did validate the missing transitions. `_BUILD_SOURCE/probe_warden_phase_gates_0925.py` followed the intro into the leg-planted chase and opening leaps, hit the 75% stun and broke one exposed core, then crossed the 50% hyper and 25% leg-burst gates. Phase screenshots and the zero-error Chromium report are under `_shots/warden_phase_gates_0925/`. This is a scripted damage progression with invulnerability; it does not establish the natural fight's duration or player escape safety.

The actual Stage 3 Furious nuclear handoffs were then checked separately. `_BUILD_SOURCE/probe_stage3_thermo_0925.py` followed the Frost Cruiser into the strike and Thermocloud Eye, then the Rime Wall into the strike and Therno. Both replacements entered the fight and fired: the miniboss made 9 shots across three opening cycles, and the boss made 33. The sampled projectiles included magma, ice mortar/orb and ice shards. The enlarged boss reticle occupies half the playfield width, and the new boss plate clears the top HUD. Screenshots and a zero-error browser report are under `_shots/stage3_thermo_0925/`. These counts still do not measure a player dodging the attacks; that is the remaining fairness check, especially the Thermocloud's flying dive and Therno's radial pressure after modules break.

The extended Frost Cruiser trace sampled 35 seconds on Normal and Hard. Normal opened with ice-orb charges, then missiles, an edge-to-edge nose-laser pass and a 5.2-second beam sweep. Hard opened with warned spiral rockets, moved into the nose-laser pass earlier, then reached its own 5.2-second sweep; its first ice orb came after that beam. Each mode fired 20 missiles and about 18–20 nose-laser rounds in the sample; Normal created more orb shards (36 versus 12), which explains why raw shot counts alone overstated its difficulty. Furious was observed only through the intentional nuclear takeover, when Thermocloud replaced the cruiser. The visual review found Normal/Hard were still drawing the old green Jungle Cruiser beam. They now use the existing authored blue ice beam on every difficulty, and the corrected Hard beam screenshot was inspected under `_shots/frost_cruiser_timeline_0925/`. All three probe modes had zero browser errors. The trace used an invulnerable pilot and does not establish beam escape fairness.

The Sovereign's four shield nodes were then destroyed through the live node-break routine and its next 35 seconds observed on each difficulty. All three modes went through burst, warned ram and lightning rather than remaining on shield orbs. Normal launched 10 chain bolts over two cycles; Hard and Furious launched 18. Furious also launched six chain orbs and entered two giant-strike sequences, while Normal and Hard had two chain orbs and no giant strike. The shielded opening's 19/21/20 counts therefore understate the post-break escalation. The yellow giant-strike warning and the Hard lightning entry were inspected in `_shots/sovereign_open_phase_0925/`, with zero browser errors. The probe deliberately broke the nodes at full HP and kept the pilot invulnerable; it does not cover the later shield rearm gates or whether the warning gives a moving player a reliable escape.

The later shield gates were then followed through the live damage router. At 75% the node field reformed; at 50% two independent helper turrets joined it. That gate now starts a rotating two-gun deployment using the existing authored core and gatling plates. In Chromium, all three difficulties reached `active` after the 25% rearm and fired 64 gun rounds in the sampled late window; before the fix they fired none. The three difficulty reports and inspected screenshots are under `_shots/sovereign_rearm_0925/`, with zero browser errors. These scripted gate-crossings prove the phase exists, but a played run is still needed to tune how its fire combines with the surviving helpers.

A paired four-second Furious pressure check then compared the active final guns with those guns disabled while retaining both helper turrets. The final guns raise the cost of standing still without adding hits on the sampled moving paths; keep their current cadence pending a real-player dodge assessment.

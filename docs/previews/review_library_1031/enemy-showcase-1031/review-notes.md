These are isolated samples, not difficulty/balance validation or complete animation-bank coverage. Fast native sorties may exit without dying. Mine/support/contact roles may not emit ordinary gunfire. Audio presence and decoded native cue events are verified; this is not a full loudness or mix audit.

3 clips have no separately identified enemy sound cue in the capture telemetry; music/audio remains present. 17 clips have no counted native shot/beam event in this isolated sample (telemetry does not count every custom emitter). These observations are review flags, not automatic gameplay defects.

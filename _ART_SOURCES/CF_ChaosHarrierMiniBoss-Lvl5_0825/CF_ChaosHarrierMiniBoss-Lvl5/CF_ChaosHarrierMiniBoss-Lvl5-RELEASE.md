# CF_ChaosHarrierMiniBoss-Lvl5

Production pack for the Level 5 Chaos Harrier space mini-boss.

- 6 fixed-canvas ship state frames at 352x320
- 12 projectile frames at 192x192
- 12 detached muzzle/charge VFX frames at 224x224
- 8 teleport-warp frames at 512x512, with mirrored warp-in/out timelines
- 4 reactor-laser frames at 192x384, 4 twin-laser frames, and 4 impact frames
- Hard-alpha runtime PNGs with zero hidden RGB
- Exact #FF00FF normalized sources plus untouched generated sources
- Seven engine-neutral AI/animation/attack/teleport/difficulty/validation JSON files
- Atlases, maps, GIF reviews, hardpoints, hitbox recommendation, and combat-preview GIF
- Ship images are state frames; only the hover pair is a loop
- Dedicated breakup/death explosion art remains a future companion pack; the AI emits a death-sequence event hook

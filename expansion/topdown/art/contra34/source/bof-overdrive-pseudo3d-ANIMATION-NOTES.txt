BULLETS OF FURY - OVERDRIVE / ARTICULATED WEAPON UPDATE

Open weapon-motion.html. Select a full cycle or an individual action. Half/quarter speed and frame stepping are provided. Uncheck Projectiles to see the mechanism clearly.

Added in this update
- 24-frame forward draws for all nine soldier families and Fire Prism.
- Arm and grip articulation, independent weapon motion and visible firing recoil.
- Minigun: six rotating bores, a rotating rim index, moving ammunition belt, 24-frame spin-up, firing and spin-down clips; twelve alternating discharges per firing loop.
- Pump shotgun: 48-frame lower, reach belt, shell insert, pump rack and return to aim sequence.
- Double barrel: 64-frame hinge opening, two spent casings, loading-hand travel, two-shell reload, closing and latching sequence.
- Wing movement and recoil during bat firing, independent turret barrel/lens retraction, and Fire Prism arm bracing during its forward chest volley.
- Reverse lowering clips derived from the PixelLab draw frames, allowing the full preview cycle to return to rest.

Timing and import
New authored clips use 60 ms per frame (16.6667 fps). Clip JSON files include the common cell/origin, release_frames and per-frame muzzle positions. Release frames are zero-based. weapon_cycle in each manifest asset is the ordered demonstration sequence; repeated entries repeat that action. All attacks and projectiles travel toward the viewer along camera depth.

The new drawings and layered animation were rendered in PixelLab's pixel-art workbench. Local tools extract the returned frames, pad to the common export cells, reverse the draw sequence for lowering, and assemble previews/atlases. No per-frame character resizing is used.

These are exported art assets and previews. Full pseudo-3D soldier locomotion, prone, crouch, jump and cover conversions remain a separate pass; this update addresses the requested weapon mechanics. The live game repository is not modified.

# Enemy-owned targeting retina brief

Built-in image generation produced the preserved generated/enemy-retina.png master. The brief requested a 4 by 4 sequence of sixteen thin industrial target optics: amber/orange scanning, tracking/closing corners, scarlet committed lock, then gray cooling. A completely empty central aperture must preserve the player's visibility. Transparent background, consistent centered registration, pixel-art edges, no lettering or grid.

The generated raw master includes unwanted faint center/outside alpha residue. The selected production export removes that residue, retains only the designed annular components and uses binary alpha. All sixteen center apertures are audited as empty. The source master remains unmodified. No PixelLab credits were used.

Aircraft, modules, laser/muzzle pixels and plume pixels are existing approved game art. Flight headings, distance sizes, hit partners and silhouette shadows are prepared offline from those masters. The 1,136 exported frames include materials, headings, sizes and effects; they are not 1,136 new aircraft drawings.

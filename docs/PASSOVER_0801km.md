## 0. READ THIS FIRST — the traps that keep biting

Carried forward from the previous passover because every one of them bit again
this session:

1. **A harness pass is not a game pass.** The suite's mock reports `complete=true`
   and `64x64` for *every* image, so a draw path can look perfectly healthy while
   emitting garbage geometry. This session that was worked around by building
   `_BUILD_SOURCE/probe_sxpack_0801kl.js`, which boots the real code with real PNG
   dimensions and *records* every `drawImage` call, then replays them into a PNG.
   Use it. Extend it. Do not trust a green suite about anything visual.
2. **Art registered ≠ art drawn ≠ art warmed.** All eight miniboss bodies were
   registered in 0801hm and orphaned until 0801kk. The crackle fix found the same
   shape again (§1).
3. **The harness must load the same scripts as `index.html`.** It did not. Fixed
   this session — see §4.
4. **A sine is not a ramp.** Smoke drift uses `(t*k)%1`, not `Math.sin`, so a plume
   never repeats its own values.
5. **Thresholds tuned to one sample generalise badly.** The magenta key on the new
   crates is ratio-based for exactly this reason (§7).

## 1. THE CRACKLE IS GONE (drop 0801kl)

> Mike: "these bosses should never do that crackle effect at all."

**It was never a runtime effect.** There was no flag, no draw call, nothing to
switch off. The zigzag bolts were **painted into the PNGs** by the 0801hm body
builder, one flat fully-opaque colour per family:

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| nlgt `(225,70,55)` | nobd `(255,112,20)` | nmrv `(255,170,42)` | nglr `(42,180,255)` |
| nslc `(144,255,35)` | nrmp `(225,54,54)` | ntxl `(118,255,36)` | ncyc `(42,162,240)` |

Three checks before touching a pixel:
* alpha is 255 on every bolt pixel — no soft edge
* a 1px ring around the bolts contains **zero** near-bolt colours — no glow halo,
  no anti-aliasing, so an exact-colour key cannot leave a fringe
* the colour appears in **no** `*_intact` frame — so obsidian's magma vents and
  glacier's energy rails are a *different* colour and were never at risk

**119,594 bolt pixels removed across 393 files. Verified 0 remaining.**
Strokes crossing the hull are inpainted from their neighbours so each state keeps
its own scorch texture; strokes arcing through open air are erased, because there
was never any body there.

Tool: `_BUILD_SOURCE/strip_crackle_0801kl.py`
Originals: `_superseded/crackle_0801kl/` (9.6 MB)

**`destruction_overlay_01..08` is pure crackle** for every family. It is
deliberately NOT wired to anything. Smoke replaces it. Do not "fix" it by hooking
it back up.

## 2. THE BODIES NOW DRAW AS SECTIONS (drop 0801kl)

> Mike: "the level 3 boss the turrets become cut frames/sectional turrets you can break."

### The bug
`sxDraw`'s new-body path (added 0801kk) drew ONE `assembled_*` frame chosen off
total hp and **returned early** — walking straight past `sxHit`, `SX_WEAPON` and
the entire sectional system. Turrets were taking damage and dying without ever
visibly coming off, because a flat body plate was painted over the top of them.

### Why compositing is safe
The section plates are **disjoint** (they tile the body, zero overlap) and
compositing the intact plates at their measured offsets reproduces
`assembled_intact` **pixel-exactly**: MAD 0.00, 100% coverage, 0 missing, 0 extra,
on all eight families. Verified again through the *live draw path* at native scale:
**MAD 0.0000, 100.00% coverage.**

So a sectional body is not an approximation of the assembled body — it is the same
pixels. The change costs nothing visually and makes every section breakable.

### What was added
* `sxPackDraw()` — composites the body, per-section damage state, per-section
  flash, per-section smoke. Returns false and falls through to the legacy path if
  geometry or plates are missing.
* `sxPackState()` — **four** states. `sxPartState` reports three because the old
  `nsx_` set has no `damaged` tier; these packs ship a real one for every section.
  `sxPartState` was left alone — other units depend on its three-state answer.
* `assets/section_geom.js` (`window.BOFSG`) — measured offsets, plate sizes,
  centroids, smoke anchors. Generated by
  `_BUILD_SOURCE/emit_section_geom_0801kl.py`. **Do not hand-edit.**

### Rules preserved
* **A destroyed section is not drawn.** (rule from 0724ce) Drawing its `destroyed`
  plate on a shared body reads as a square changing colour rather than a turret
  being blown off.
* **`odt`/`grf` now name the eight sections the art actually has** — `hull`,
  `turret_core`, `left/right_weapon_pod`, `front_weapon`, `rear_engine`,
  `left/right_track` — instead of six invented names (`core_hull`,
  `left_rail_turret`, `ice_ram`) that existed nowhere in the packs. Six became
  eight; that is why the turrets can now come off.

### An approach that was tried and thrown away
Force-cropping the damage plates onto the intact canvas. It is **lossy** — a
destroyed plate is legitimately *larger* than its intact one (debris spreads), and
the crop ate 232 real pixels of `ntxl_tail_blade_destroyed`. Nothing is resized
now; `assets/section_offsets.json` carries per-state placement instead.

## 3. FLASH AND SMOKE (drop 0801kl)

> Mike: "they just flash when you attack, as they take damage, you can overlay our
> smoke frames in sections"

* **Flash** — `sxHit` stamps `S.flash[part] = 0.09`, so only the plate actually
  shot lights up, instead of the whole tank washing white. Same correction the
  quadlaser's cannons got in 0801kg.
* **Smoke** — `smk_0..5` (the 62x88 rising column in `assets/fx/smoke/`), anchored
  at the **top-centre of each section's own opaque mass** so it vents off the
  section rather than blooming from its middle. Damaged → 1 plume, critical → 2,
  destroyed → 3.

**Smoke was tuned once already.** The first pass sized every plume at `b.w*0.30`
and rose it `rise*sc*2.2` — up to 74px on a 237px body, which floated the smoke
clear off the top of the tank and made damage read as weather. It is now sized
from `sec.r` (the section's own measured radius) and capped to a short climb.
Do not re-widen it without looking at the render.

## 4. HIT ATTRIBUTION IS NOW MEASURED, NOT GUESSED (drop 0801kl)

`SX_ANCHOR` is a table of hand-estimated fractions, and `sxHit` uses it to decide
**which section a shot landed on** — so the estimates were deciding whether
shooting a turret hurt the turret. Checked against the art:

| part | guessed | measured | error |
|---|---|---|---|
| drill ram | `y 0.34` | `y 0.21` | 13% of the body |
| tracks | `x -0.32` | `x -0.42` | 10% |

`sxPartOffset` now reads the measured centroid for the eight packs. **Every other
unit still falls through to `SX_ANCHOR` unchanged.** Probe result: **8/8** — every
section's centroid damages that exact section.

**The harness was not loading `section_geom.js`.** It booted a different program
from the browser. `run('assets/section_geom.js')` was added in the same order as
`index.html`. If you add another top-level script, add it in both places.

## 7. THE FOUR MISSILE CRATES (drop 0801km)

Art: `nmc_20` `nmc_35` `nmc_50` `nmc_100` in `assets/pickups/crates/`.

**Chroma key.** The source magenta is *not* flat — measured `(224,3,233)`
`(225,3,233)` `(217,1,231)` `(205,2,224)`, compressed and noisy, so an exact-RGB
match leaves a magenta rind. The key is **ratio-based**: green collapses while red
and blue both stay high, plus `|R-B|` small. That last clause is what protects the
art — these crates are full of RED (missile stripes, the ELITE stamp), and red is
high-R / low-G / **low-B**, so it fails the test. Only the region
**flood-connected to the border** is removed, so an interior magenta-ish pixel is
kept. Verified by compositing on green: no rind.
Tool: `_BUILD_SOURCE/key_crates_0801km.py`

**Sizing is by AREA, not height** (`MSL_BIG[].a`). The four have wildly different
aspects — the x20 is a tall locker (63x128), the x35 a wide flight case (288x128).
A shared height made the 20 draw at 953 px² — *smaller* than the existing 2-pack
at 1352 — while the 35 came out at 2601. Tiers now run 1800 / 2150 / 2550 / 3300,
all above the 2/5/10 packs they outrank.

**Spawn gating** — `MSL_STAGE_TIERS`, one line per stage:
```
6: ['missilepack20','missilepack35']
8: ['missilepack50']
```
`MSL_BIG_CHANCE = 0.28` that a missile crate upgrades to its stage's big tier.
`mslPackRoll()` is the **single** place that decides — the breakable supply box and
the `mcrate` pickup each used to carry their own copy of the 50/30/20 weights.

**The 100 is cinematic-only.** It is deliberately absent from `MSL_STAGE_TIERS` and
is handed out by `grantCinematicMissiles(x,y)`. A harness assertion proves it can
never appear from a stage roll. **`grantCinematicMissiles` is not called anywhere
yet — the end cinematic has to call it.**

### ⚠ OPEN QUESTION — the stage 8 rule
> "on levels 8, 25-50 will spawn"

This does not resolve cleanly against a 20/35/50/100 set: **25 is not one of the
four**, and **35 is already spoken for by "levels 6 only"**. Taken literally, the
only crate inside a 25–50 band once 35 is excluded is the 50 — so that is what
stage 8 got. **If the intent was "35 and 50 on stage 8", it is one line in
`MSL_STAGE_TIERS` and nothing else changes.**

## 8. A REAL BUG FOUND AND FIXED (drop 0801km)

```js
case 'bomb':
  run.bombs = clamp(run.bombs+1, 0, 9);   // ← was
```

The pack cases beside it clamped to 99; this one clamped to **9**. Holding 20
missiles and touching a single-missile pickup ran `clamp(21,0,9)` and **threw away
12 of them — a pickup could LOSE you ammo.** Harmless while 10 was the largest
crate and stock rarely passed 9; ruinous now a 50 crate exists.

All missile clamps now share `MSL_CAP` (999) so they cannot drift apart again.
`run.lives` keeps its own `0..9` — that one is a real design cap, not this bug.
The HUD prints `'x'+run.bombs` as text at all three draw sites, so three digits
render with no layout change.

BULLETS OF FURY - OVERDRIVE
PixelLab enemy animation and weapon effects pack

OPEN preview.html to play, pause, step through frames, change speed, face
left, and compare animation states. The viewer works from this folder.

ROSTER
Cobalt Machine Gunner - cobalt blue; belt-fed machine gun.
Ochre Minigunner - yellow ochre; heavy rotary minigun and ammo pack.
Jade Sniper - green; long scoped rifle and shoulder mantle.
Vermilion Rocketeer - red; shoulder rocket launcher.
Violet Breacher - purple; pump-action shotgun.
Bronze Twin Barrel - bronze; short double-barrel shotgun.
Cyan Skyrake - teal/cyan; winged alien with forearm plasma emitter.
Crimson Armadroid - crimson; plated alien that folds into a rolling ball.

CHARACTERS
Each character folder contains transparent right/left sprite sheets and
matching JSON files. Each animation cell is 128 x 128 pixels. The shared
origin is (64,112). Art remains at its native pixel scale inside the cell;
armor bulk and standing height vary by enemy. Left sheets mirror the right.

Core sequences: walk, run, standing fire, jump, crouch-and-fire,
prone-and-fire, wall-cover-and-fire, combat roll, and a static ready pose.
Crouch and prone pose PNGs are included for holding those states.
The flying alien adds takeoff/landing, flying and firing while flying.
The roller alien adds transform-to-ball, ball-roll and reverse transform.

Crouch, prone, jump, cover and roll sheets are complete action sequences.
The wall itself is a viewer overlay; no scenery is baked into the actor.
The ball-to-humanoid transition reverses the same transformation frames so
both endpoints and armor plates match. The roller's combat-roll sequence
joins folding, rolling and unfolding.

EFFECTS
Open effects/index.json for the eight effect kits and their individual
clips. Each kit's clips.json describes frame size, frame count, frame rate,
looping and origin. Every normalized clip has right/left PNG sheets.
The parts/ folder and original game ZIP preserve PixelLab's source export.

Rifle/minigun: tracer, muzzle flash, brass casing and metal impact.
Sniper: long tracer, muzzle glint and precise impact.
Shotgun: single pellet, broad muzzle flash, smoke and scatter impact.
Double barrel: pellet, twin muzzle flash, smoke and red shell.
Rocket: rocket projectile, exhaust trail, launch flash and explosion.
Flying alien: cyan plasma bolt, forked muzzle flare and plasma impact.
Roller alien: lime acid globule, spit flare and acid splat.
Movement: rolling dust, landing dust and armor sparks.

Spawn multiple instances of the pellet sprite for a shotgun spread.
Move projectiles through the game; travel is not baked into their sheets.

IMPORT
Use nearest-neighbor texture filtering and preserve transparency. Frame
rectangles and durations are in the JSON. Gameplay movement, collision,
weapon timing, cover logic and damage logic belong to the game. Jump and
tumble motion is drawn inside the cells. Check attachment points and
movement together in the target engine when integrating.

The pack contains original PixelLab-generated designs using the supplied
arcade references for visual direction. It does not contain reference
screenshots. The generated sources remain in your PixelLab Creator gallery;
the effect kits are saved in My effects for later editing.

# CF_EnemyShieldFX-Vol.1

Reusable enemy force fields, directional deflectors, and detached hit/ricochet effects for Bullets of Fury.

- 3 omnidirectional force fields: Ion Bubble, Crimson Plasma Field, Violet Phase Shell
- 3 forward deflectors: Forward Hex Deflector, Gold Kinetic Mirror, Alien Prism Deflector
- 48 shield state frames at 384x384
- Registered back/front layers for all full force fields
- 12 detached impact and ricochet frames at 256x256
- Teal #008080 and cyan #00FFFF normalized source keys selected by effect palette
- Hard-alpha runtime frames, atlases, GIF previews, maps, and engine-neutral behavior JSON

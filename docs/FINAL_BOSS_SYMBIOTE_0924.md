# Stage 8 final boss: symbiote possession sequence

Status: four-form runtime, possession entrance, separate modules, attacks, shield shedding, 16-frame player void death, ground portal and rising beam integrated locally on 2026-09-24. Real Chromium captures show the sequence and beam collision. No commit or push requested. This specification records the intended order and counters so the new visuals do not outrun a fair encounter.

1. A gray machine arrives from the existing robot plate. A black portal opens behind it, fragments encircle it, the eye looks left and right, the arms rise, and the symbiote consumes the machine. Only then is form I damageable.
2. Form I: black machine/alien fusion, *separate* torso and left/right rocket-attachment arms. Destroying an arm visibly removes that arm and disables its rocket lane. Center cannon remains the only laser source.
3. Form II: more organic body with surviving gray fragments, separate cyan cannon arm and rocket-claw arm. Each can be disabled independently; damage does not restore them until the next form.
4. Form III: face-hidden rolling symbiote ball, using the six approved rolling plates. The plates are preloaded and the last decoded frame remains visible while a new frame decodes. Its moves are ricochets and controlled radial releases, leaving a visible safe lane.
5. Form IV: the approved fully alien face/tendril plate, with dark-void grabs, tendril spokes and alternating laser/rocket pressure. No new machine claws grow back.

Each attack is warned, commits to a snapshot of the target, releases, and recovers before the next primary attack. Weakpoints are always hittable during recovery. Bullets should be readable against the Stage 8 starfield; the symbiote shield should be translucent, shed decals on hits, and break after a bounded damage pool. Rocket pods and laser muzzles flash at their physical anchors. A dark-void hit that actually kills a player animates the selected player's ship into a 16-frame, ship-free overlay: small circle, expanding coils, crush, dark-matter burst, contraction and disappearance. Shield absorption still follows the existing player rules.

Visual source files: `final_boss_concepts/obsidian_form_0924` and `final_boss_0924`. The 16-frame circular and ground-rise portal sheets use 4x4 cells on 1448x1086 RGBA plates. The ground-rise sheet is for the void attack/intro on a playfield plane; it is distinct from the circular player-death overlay. No purple or baked ship appears in either final portal plate.

Design research: GDC's [Boss Up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48Ymh4Ge5j8) emphasizes bosses as a culmination of previously learned skills; Double Fine's [Psychonauts 2 boss-design slides](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2023/Slides/CraftingEpicBoss_Vessal_Beca.pdf) define the telegraph, attack, recovery loop; the [Luna Abyss developer interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/how-unreal-engine-empowered-a-small-team-to-build-luna-abyss-beautiful-bullet-hell-prison) explains how slow, predictable bullet behavior and audible offscreen tells support intense patterns; [EXOR's X-Morph Bomber retrospective](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/11-boss-battles-of-x-morph-defense---part-3-the-bomber) illustrates why destructible weapon systems and preventable attacks are more satisfying than uncontrolled missile spam. These are design references, not instructions from the attachments.

The ground portal beam is a separate 8-frame overlay. On a void attack, the portal first warns the locked ground point, a vertical lane appears, and the beam grows upward from the burst. A late straight-up lift within that lane can be fatal; lateral escape before and during extension stays possible.

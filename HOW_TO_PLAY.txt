BULLETS OF FURY
ColeForge


TO PLAY
-------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere, keeping it together.
2. Double-click  index.html

It opens in your browser and runs straight from the file - no installer, no server
to start, nothing to sign up for. Chrome, Edge and Firefox are all fine.

Keep the "assets" folder next to index.html. Move index.html on its own and you
get a black screen, because everything it draws lives in that folder.


CONTROLS
--------
  W A S D  or  arrow keys     fly
  J                           fire   (hold it down)
  K                           bomb / special
  C                           retina lock-on

  Enter, Space or J           confirm
  Esc, Backspace or K         back

A gamepad works too if one is plugged in. The keys can be rebound in Options.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS OFF
----------------------
The first load takes a few seconds while the art decodes. After that it is quick.


A work-in-progress build. Nine stages, nine pilots, the art all hand-drawn.

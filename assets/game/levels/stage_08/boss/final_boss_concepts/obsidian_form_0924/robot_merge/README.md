# Final boss first form — alien robot fusion

Mike selected the gray robot merged with the black symbiote as the final boss's first-form direction on 2026-09-24. `fusion_black.png` is the current darker concept. All generated images are 1254×1254 RGBA with transparent backgrounds.

- `robot_source_magenta.png`: Mike's robot source image.
- `robot_gray.png`: cool gunmetal/steel palette swap of the source, retaining its head, single green eye, cannons, jointed arms, and claws.
- `fusion_gray.png`: initial fusion of the gray robot with `../obsidian_alien_face.png`.
- `fusion_black.png`: revised fusion with most bright silver armor darkened to blackened steel, graphite, and obsidian, retaining readable face, cannons, joints, and claws.

Built-in imagegen prompts preserved the original robot anatomy and 16-bit pixel-art style. The fusion prompt introduced an alien face emerging through torn front armor, black chitin and tendrils integrated with the mechanical parts, restrained green optic, and sparse dark-red fissures. The final edit darkened the metal without changing pose or anatomy. These are concept plates; gameplay registration and animation frames remain to be implemented.

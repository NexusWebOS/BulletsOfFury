OCTOBER 31, 2026 / CURRENT FIELD NOTES

The war continues on Hard or Furious.

Normal finishes after Stage 5 / Chronos. Your clear autosaves, you return to Earth, then choose Hard or Furious and reselect pilots for a fresh Stage 1 operation. Furious is the intended experience and unlocks the true ending on campaign completion.

  • Stage 7 / Furious: two Sumpjaws, separate overhead health bars; destroy each pair of gills before its heart. Hard retains the Dredger.
  • Event Horizon: continuous shared rotation, warned laser pulses with escape gaps, earlier dash corridor and protected re-entry. Solo health: Hard 3,520 / Furious 4,160.
  • Nightwing: following ace life is 3,960 HP on Hard / 4,680 on Furious. The carrier's modular hull gates remain.
  • Tidal Sovereign: Hard uses 3 forward spread lanes, 3 mortars, 4 rain impacts and 6 lamp bubbles; Furious uses 5 / 4 / 6 / 10. Both retain all three water-wall alerts and wave sounds.

These single-pool HP figures receive native co-op ×1.5 and NG+ ×1.25 where applicable. Older illustrations below remain reference images; current attack timing and phase rules are authoritative in the game.

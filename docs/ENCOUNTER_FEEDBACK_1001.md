## Limits and next playtest

These are controlled encounter fixtures with invulnerability and scripted positions, not full campaign wins or proof of difficulty balance. Stage 8's 40-second samples do not cover its entire late-form attack book. Timing samples were collected while other local checks ran and are not a stable FPS benchmark. No measured human time-to-kill is claimed. The highest-value playtest is the Stage 8 full transition chain, the shorter but more aggressive bombers, and the Furious Warden's complete leg-to-core fight. Adjust from those playtests instead of declaring the game balanced from health values alone.

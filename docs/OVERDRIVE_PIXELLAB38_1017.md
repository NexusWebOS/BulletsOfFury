# PixelLab38 — reviewed art integration

Local build `overdrive1017-pixellab38-r1`, October 17. No commit or push.

The active Overdrive entry now loads art/pixellab38/manifest.js and js/pixellab38.js. It adds 149 banks / 1,352 playback frames in 154 PNG sheets/plates. This count includes quiet ready holds, native VFX and explicitly derived directional exports; it is not a count of new AI generations or fully replaced gameplay actions. PixelLab is the source provider. Existing encounter layouts, collisions, controls, tactics, one-hit rules, lives and weapon damage are preserved.

Prism Bastion uses reviewed forward-facing firing banks for the machine gunner, minigunner, sniper, rocket launcher, pump shotgunner, double barrel, flamethrower, flyer and roller. Native six-port minigun spin-up/fire/spin-down share their fixed hardware. Pump discharge occurs at native frame 2; the double barrel releases at frames 2 and 10, then uses the 37-frame break-action reload. New clocks play the complete bank instead of resetting with each bullet. Attack interruption cancels future rounds. Existing approved gun cues use the shared mixer. Gun turrets alternate their two actual barrels on the same frame events; dead turrets cancel queued rounds. Active/inactive cyan door cores preserve the metal casing. Actual damaged pixels retain the existing white-flash path.

Native projectiles and muzzle effects use their declared origins and timing. Each shotgun pellet draws one projectile; the whole discharge sheet is never stamped on each separate pellet. Projectile collisions and existing forward travel remain owned by the game. Viewport/camera scaling still applies uniformly to whole actors; there is no independent width/height stretch or per-frame size fitting. Exports bake the chosen fixed bank/body unit and drawScale is 1.0.

Yuri's clean side walk/run/fire/crawl/roll/low holds are imported, with lossless whole-bank mirrored facings; the active corridor selector uses the compatible states and preserves existing specialized aiming, cover, jumps and slides. North-facing basic top-down poses use the reviewed new sources, with existing directional/specialized fallbacks elsewhere. Gosfer now shares one new whole-plate pose selector between drawing and the weapon socket for compatible movement and fire. His existing dialogue/transformation/death behavior remains. Helios uses the new reviewed forward lightning cast; incomplete boss banks remain excluded.

Level 3 uses the PixelLab floor material and active/inactive three-relay electrical gate while retaining the 800x2800 route and collision plan. The original floor layout remains available. The elevator sequence uses the generated floor-hatch opening frames through its complete 1.28-second bank. The service corridor uses independent PixelLab backdrop and foreground layers around the existing physical props/passages. The level 4 native perspective environment continues to own its camera and depth; its new background/foreground plates are imported for later reviewed placement.

Lazy loading selects a new attack controller at a cycle boundary. A bank becoming ready during an old volley cannot take over a partially initialized event queue. `?pixelLab=0` retains the prior runtime paths as an explicit comparison/fallback.

## Stage 4

Regenerate the exact highway sequence with repeating matching roadside trees so its infinite loop has no jump. Boss death: four smoke rings in the horizontal/vertical/+45/-45 arrangement; major blast followed by progressively faster explosions OVER the intact falling ship. Slow downward fall; remove ship under white flash, return to normal with residual blasts. No morph of the ship into explosion art. The exit must not interrupt the sequence.

## Stage 5 Easy/Normal replacement

Shelf the existing Xenoregent for possible later elite use on Stage 9 or Stage 6. Do not place it there yet. Preserve current Hard behavior unless Mike changes that scope.

Use SpriteCook. Original living-metal transforming robot combining chrome cosmic metal, superhero armor and heroic transforming-machine proportions, wielding a two-handed alien energy hammer. Ship enters from bottom harmlessly, genuinely passes offscreen rather than orbiting beside the player, re-enters from top, brakes and hovers in upper portion (~35% screen Y; interpret 65% up from bottom). Authored transformation into robot.

Normal hammer form; red rage charge reel; blue shield charge reel; curl/transformation to black spiked ball. Ball lasts roughly 15 seconds: charged entry, initially slow, physically reflects at all screen edges. Standard weapon hits enrage into faster red/black form with animated spike laser attacks. Difficult but telegraphed attacks that reward barrel rolls, somersaults and movement; requested one-in-four survival is a difficulty aspiration, not a measured probability.

Manual missile-button missiles knock ball back without enraging; passive weapon homing missiles must not qualify. During this fight allow only x10/x20 missile supplies, disable x5. Return transformation into hammer form after ball timer. Hammer charge has bottom-to-top pixel flash, green/yellow/red target warning reticles, leap to committed player location, then jump backward to station or retarget player vicinity and leap again. Long-range weapons and manual missile management are central.

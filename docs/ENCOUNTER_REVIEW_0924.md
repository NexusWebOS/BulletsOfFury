# Furious encounter review and September 24 changes

This is a direct real-Chromium encounter review, not a complete Stage 1–2 playthrough. The probes spawned each miniboss or boss against the live renderer, AI, projectiles, HUD, and audio routes, then captured successive seconds under `_shots/`. They establish the visual cadence and attack readability at the boss; they do not measure player win rate, survival, or the entire stage approach.

| Encounter | What the Furious capture shows | Design rule carried forward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stage 1 Razorback miniboss | A large, immediately identifiable tank and discrete sonic charge/warning beats (`_shots/s1_fury_mini_0924`). | Announce a dangerous move with one clear source before increasing projectile count. |
| Stage 1 Jungle Overlord-X | The helicopter banks across the player's lane while gunfire and rockets keep the lower field active (`_shots/s1_fury_boss_0924`). | Make movement and aiming interact, then leave a dodge window when the boss commits. |
| Stage 2 Magma Ward miniboss | Its fire identity reads from the hull and the attack lane, even against a dense volcanic field (`_shots/s2_fury_mini_0924`). | Keep the projectile origin anchored to the weapon and preserve a recognizable escape corridor. |
| Stage 2 Inferno Reaver | A charge and shield moment separate the heavy release from recovery (`_shots/s2_fury_boss_0924`). | Large effects need a distinct warning, release, and punish window. |

Stage 3 Normal now teaches the Frost Cruiser's charge-and-burst ice orb immediately, then cycles into its existing missiles, flyby, gun run, and beam. Hard still opens with the six alternating spiral rockets; the orb remains in its rotation. Rime Wall now interleaves a short side-battery bracket and aimed core shot with its longer siege phases on Normal and Hard. On Hard the core locks through a warning and fires at the sampled position; after release, a skilled player can dodge laterally. The earlier Hard-tracking call sat after a laser handler that returned first, so its lock never ran on those attacks. The call is now before that handler and remains active after half health. Furious retains the authored Thermocloud Eye and Therno replacement calendar rather than inheriting the Normal/Hard detour. Focused captures are in `_shots/s3_normal_mini_focused_0924`, `_shots/s3_hard_mini_focused_0924`, `_shots/s3_normal_hunt_0924`, `_shots/s3_hard_hunt_0924`, `_shots/s3_fury_mini_0924`, and `_shots/s3_fury_boss_0924`.

Stage 7's Toxic Portal Warden now follows its portal arrival with a slow reverse-scroll chase, four planted leg strokes, a faster closing run, and two opening leaps (three on Furious). Leaps also recur in the regular pattern rotation. Each has a warning, target commitment, landing impact, and return before the next leap. The longer live capture `_shots/s7_chase_long_0924` reaches the chase, leap, and normal combat handoff. The boss is huge and can obscure the central lane; its warning and attack direction should remain visible during further balance playtests.

After the Stage 6 Harrier route, five rival contacts scatter from Stage 7 onto map regions 2, 3, 4, 5, and 8. Selecting any contact shows a new Rival Fight card and an authored ally window. The player chooses two other available pilots; their actual ships then follow, intercept shots, and shoot at the five-ship Rival Crew. The selected contact supplies the arena's authored terrain. Clearing this five-member encounter removes all five markers and returns to the campaign map. Stage 8's dialogue and scripted hazards do not run in the optional fight. Browser captures cover map, ally selection, live battle, selected Stage 8 terrain, and return-to-map in `_shots/rival_*_0924`.

The selected SpriteCook Cover B follows the ColeForge boot in the opener with the existing Bullets of Fury logo layered over it. The prerecorded gameplay beats are removed. The live image is `_shots/cover_b_final_0924/shot_0001.png`.

Next balance pass should use an unassisted full campaign run on each difficulty, with player hit/death telemetry and successful dodges per boss pattern. The direct-spawn captures cannot establish whether all warning windows remain fair with normal weapon levels, deaths, and stage transitions.

BULLETS OF FURY - COMPLETE WINDOWS GAME + FURY HQ LAUNCHER / REV F

EASIEST INSTALL
1. Download BOF-Install-Helper-RevF.zip and extract it to a normal folder.
2. Double-click Install-BulletsOfFury.cmd.
3. The helper downloads both large release parts, checks their SHA256 hashes,
   joins and verifies the original complete installer, then opens Fury HQ setup.

You may also download both .part files manually into that extracted helper
folder. The helper will verify and reuse them. Keep the .cmd and .ps1 together.
The two .part files cannot run independently. About12GB free disk space is
needed for the downloaded parts, assembled installer and installed game.

After installation:
- Bullets of Fury.exe starts the game automatically in fullscreen.
- BOF Launcher.exe opens the animated Fury HQ launcher.
- All game graphics, music, sounds and the offline WebView2 runtime are included.
- Windows10/11 x64; no Python, Node or separate browser is required to play.

Retina:right-click. Missiles:Space. Multi-Retina:Mouse4 or double-right-hold.
The helper keeps your downloaded files and does not touch your saved games.
Normal saves remain in LocalAppData/ColeForge/BulletsOfFury.

Includes the Stage5 original sky/starfield restoration and smaller assembly
pieces, performance/audio/deployment repairs, locked Cole silhouettes and
neutral fills, revised launcher credits/font/window controls, and current
modular Furious encounters/art.

Validation:focused browser/fullscreen EXE checks passed; all installer files
verified by checksum. The broader source test suite still stops at the existing
missing difficultyHealthBaseline15 helper; this is not a full-campaign test pass.

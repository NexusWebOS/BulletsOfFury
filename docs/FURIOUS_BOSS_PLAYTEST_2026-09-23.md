# Furious boss playtest: stages 1–6

Tested in real Chromium on the current `main` checkout (`06091b09`) on 2026-09-23. I opened each miniboss and boss through the game's Boss Mode entry point on Furious, advanced the real game loop, captured high/mid/low-health frames, and checked page and console errors. I also ran each boss for 20 seconds with an unarmed, stationary Cole and normal damage enabled. The stationary probe is an exposure comparison, **not** a claim that a moving, attacking player would lose the same number of lives. The health-phase captures set HP directly to exercise later patterns; they do not validate the actual damage transition or a full uninterrupted campaign clear. Audio was muted for this run.

Recommended order:

1. **Rebuild Stage 3's pressure curve before adding HP.** Give Rime Wall a repeatable, readable attack cycle that makes the player change lanes, then combine it with a second threat at low health. Use Frost Cruiser's existing lock/beam language as preparation. Keep its health near the time-to-kill target once the attacks are in place. Its present 2.18× Stage 2 health with sparse visible hazards makes it an endurance wall instead of a step up in skill.
2. **Bridge Stages 3 and 4.** Let late Stage 3 teach one of Stage 4's concepts (tracking locks or persistent field hazards), while Stage 4 introduces cores and helpers one at a time before combining them. Cap or telegraph Olive Warden's deep move into the player area; ensure its helpers remain targetable from normal weapon range.
3. **Clarify Stage 5's transformation.** Present Chromium Hammer's second 9,889-HP pool as an explicit phase break with a new health bar/name and a brief safe read. Make Chaos Harrier use its teleport windows and beams in deliberate alternation so the miniboss builds skill for the boss instead of merely adding beam width.
4. **Stage 6 should test mastered skills, not hide them.** Preserve the carrier's scale and squad chaos, but separate retina locks, escorts, and carrier fan attacks into legible beats before overlapping them. Keep a safe escape lane after the first ambush and verify the split timer/dialogue never covers active boss play or Boss Mode tests.
5. **Run a true campaign-clear balance pass.** This isolated encounter run cannot assess pickups, weapon upgrades, attrition, transition rewards, or whether a human can clear all six stages consecutively. Record player movement, weapon choice, deaths, and time-to-kill on an uninterrupted Furious route after the above changes.

Furious miniboss replacements: concepts for your review

These four boards are proposals. Their replacement encounters have not been installed.

Stage 2 · Pyroclast Wardens

Two ember/black volcanic craft. Alternating heat attacks leave a broad opening; physical magma globules can be destroyed. Each twin exposes its own core after its guns break.

Stage 3 · Cryocode Bastion

A data bomb corrupts an ice cruiser into black-ice modular armor. Destroy its crystal capacitors, read the committed ice-lance lanes, then attack the lens.

Stage 4 · Roadbreaker Aftershock

A cargo explosion tears the disguise off a straight-driving siege chassis. Four real cannon modules alternate fire; wrecked mounts open the reactor corridor.

Stage 5 · Parallax Eclipse

Two independent orbital guardians, one corrupted by Dracodia’s data capsule. They alternate laser turns and venting recoveries, with separate gun/core budgets.

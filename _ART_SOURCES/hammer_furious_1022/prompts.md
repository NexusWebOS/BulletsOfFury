# Hammer re-authoring: built-in GPT image generation

The files in `generated/` were created with the built-in image generator, with real transparency requested. PixelLab's existing windup/strike clips and original Hama double were motion references. No new PixelLab generation was charged for this pass.

Identity references: `references/blue.png` is the actual Stage5 black/chromium, blue-reactor Hammer. `references/corrupt.png` is the actual purple Dracodia skull/armor and spiked energy hammer. Each request included the corresponding identity; later requests also included the newly authored matching combo sheet.

Shared production prompt: HD16bit Neo-Geo whole-robot pixel animation, clean deliberate chromium highlights, readable face and hands, same body proportions and baseline throughout. One rigid cylindrical hammer with two flat end caps and two hands gripping its shaft. Exact reference identity. Full hands, feet, body and weapon inside every cell; transparent background; no borders, labels, scenery, motion smears, second hammer or extra limbs. Requested512-square cells; actual returned sheet sizes are recorded in the manifest's source metadata. The exporter isolates complete authored figures rather than trusting the requested grid.

## Final prompt set

- **blue-combo / corrupt-combo:** Sixteen poses, four columns/four rows. Ready; pull hammer behind shoulder; elbows/hands behind crown; maximum jumping windup; pull forward above forehead; descend across chest; descend below belt; straight-down lower-end-cap landing between feet; lift; load right; sweep left across chest; left follow-through; reverse load left; sweep right; right follow-through; ready. Avoid a staff planted in front of the face, a flat barrel strike, detached hands and variable character scale. Blue energy for Stage5; violet-white with spiked end caps for Dracodia.
- **blue-throw / corrupt-throw:** Twelve poses, four columns/three rows. First eight: ready; chest load; shoulder cock; maximum behind-head two-handed windup; forward acceleration; release with open EMPTY hands; empty follow-through; empty recovery. Last four: the SAME detached hammer only, in four orientations. No held hammer in released cells. Keep physical tool size and identity. The reviewed detached reference is pre-rendered into32 stored spin directions.
- **blue-rage / corrupt-rage:** Twelve poses, four columns/three rows. Four transformation keys: crossed-arm brace; screaming strain; fully powered scream; red armed ready. Four armed idle/charge keys with a changing jagged red aura and red-white hammer. Four unarmed screaming/brace keys with no weapon. Stage5 becomes dark crimson chromium. Dracodia additionally has red circuit paths and binary packets on its armor/helmet, with distinct packet positions across frames. Keep the face and tool readable; avoid a bubble shield, extra limbs and white explosions. Malformed armed screaming frames were excluded.
- **corrupt-redcombo:** Edit the exact combo sheet: retain every pose, grip, silhouette, layout and scale. Change armor to crimson/black chrome and hammer energy to red-white. Add thin red circuits and tiny binary glyphs embedded on armor plates, following their surfaces, with varying packet positions per frame. No added aura or pose changes.
- **corrupt-redthrow:** Edit the exact throw/tool sheet: retain every pose and released/empty hand, layout and transparency. Red/black chrome, red-white hammer, red flowing code on plates and helmet. Preserve the empty-handed cells and hammer-only final row; no extra weapons.
- **blue-sides / corrupt-sides:** Sixteen poses, four columns/four rows. A two-handed RIGHT-to-LEFT hammer arc over the first eight poses, then LEFT-to-RIGHT over the next eight. Load behind a shoulder, begin sweep, pass front-right/front-left, cross center chest, push fully to the far side, follow through with hip/shoulder twist and planted pivoting boots, reverse, recover. Same anatomy and hammer length, exact identity, permanent contained energy. The returned ordering was reviewed and physically reordered in the exporter.
- **blue-cross / corrupt-cross:** One complete body pose: midpoint of a leftward horizontal strike. Torso and hips twist left, hammer head at center chest in front of the body, shaft extends right with BOTH hands gripping it. Face visible above the hammer; feet planted wide; full shaft and head inside margins. Same reference armor and scale. This supplies the missing center-crossing key; the opposite crossing is an allowed sprite flip.
- **aura:** Eight effects only, four columns/two rows. Empty transparent humanoid-shaped center; jagged rising crimson/white flame corona, short red lightning fingers. Four changing plain flame frames, then four digital variants with circuit trails and tiny rising0/1 fragments. No body, weapon, ground, bubble or background. Frames are stored pixels; no browser glow or opacity animation.

## Selection and assembly

Source sheets sometimes ignored grid boundaries/direction order. Connected-figure extraction retains the complete authored robot/weapon and discards neighboring figures. Blue combo3 had a clipped upper weapon; corrupt combo1/5 shared an overlapping component. These were excluded. The final windups mix approved compatible throw/raise keys; recovery lifts to a low ready grip instead of rewinding the entire overhead windup. Each source image has one declared anatomical pixel unit and measured row baseline; poses are not individually fitted to their silhouette bounds.

All selected actor frames use448-square padded canvases and common pivot224,258; detached hammer orientations use512-square canvases around their physical head pivot256,256. Chrome, red and white flash palettes are stored assets. Original generated sheets remain unchanged in this archive.

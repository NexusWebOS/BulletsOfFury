## Recommended order

Razorwake and Sumpjaw first; Thunderhead as the larger optional boss project. For Dracodia, begin by selecting one signature pattern per form, with clear recovery rather than extra health or life bars. All proposed gaps/timing/budgets remain untested until implemented.

## RAZORWAKE — The Slipstream Hunter

STAGE 6 · ALTERNATE MINIBOSS
A compact wind interceptor that hunts you through the clouds. A faster, more physical contrast to the Siege Fortress.

[Full concept board](<C:/Users/Mike/Documents/New project/BOF-Furious-Encounter-Concepts-1031/concepts_1031/razorwake.png>)

Furious-only alternative to the Stage 6 miniboss. Keep the fight solo. Do not add it alongside the Fortress or alter the later Harrier / Rough Riders choice.

Two actual wing-root rotary cannons protect the fuselage. Proposed budget: 30% per cannon, 40% exposed body. Shots must pass through the protected body to reach either cannon. Engines and intake fans remain passive.

### Airbrake Ambush

Tell: Wing brakes spread, teal source-pixel charge and two clear gun lanes.

Action: It surges north, brakes into view and fires two committed rotary-cannon streams from the actual pods.

Counter: Move between or outside the lanes after aim commits. The barrels cannot track you during that burst.

### Knife-Edge Pass

Tell: Metal side warning and a committed crossing lane.

Action: It rolls into profile and crosses the screen with a visible floor/cloud shadow and a short, finite wind wake.

Counter: Change vertical position, roll or somersault. The exhaust is visual propulsion, not a separate weapon.

### Shear Volley

Tell: Teal emission from the dedicated wing-edge ports, matching spread FOV.

Action: It launches narrow, elongated wind blades in staggered rows, then exposes itself during the drift turn.

Counter: Read the gaps and punish the turning recovery. Wind blades are ordinary energy and cannot be shot down.

Phase change: Breaking a cannon removes that stream, its target and its warnings. When both break, the body opens and it relies on warned passes and short wind volleys.

Production art: Whole craft idle, airbrake, gun recoil, 8/16 headings, true roll/somersault/drift, two real turret modules, thruster levels, wind-blade travel/impact, depth shadows and native-style breakup.

Limit: The board illustrates the design and motion. Its bullet angles are not a tested firing solution; production must use the real pod sockets and verified escape space.

## THUNDERHEAD — Storm Containment Dreadnought

STAGE 6 · ALTERNATE BOSS
An industrial machine carrying a captive storm. Its pressure projectors divide the arena into readable routes while the reactor changes the shape of the fight.

[Full concept board](<C:/Users/Mike/Documents/New project/BOF-Furious-Encounter-Concepts-1031/concepts_1031/thunderhead.png>)

A separately chosen Furious storm contact. It should replace that run's main boss route, not force the player through Harrier, Nightwing, Rough Riders and another boss in one stage. Story/reward mapping needs approval before implementation.

Four visible gun turrets and two side pressure-projector modules protect the central reactor. All six must go before reactor exposure. The separate rear propulsion units are passive. Breaking a weapon cancels its queued attacks; no invisible emitter remains.

### Pressure Gates

Tell: The two pressure towers open mechanically and charge in alternating order.

Action: Two descending wind sheets create a broad channel, then shift their opening for the next committed release.

Counter: Enter the announced channel. It must remain reachable at the player's current speed and cannot close around an aircraft.

### Storm Rail

Tell: A bright reactor pulse and a filled, three-color warning showing the entire path.

Action: A horizontal electric front descends with one deliberately open section; the turrets remain quiet during this heavy turn.

Counter: Move into the opening before release. No mandatory last-frame dodge or projectile interception.

### Eye of the Storm

Tell: Broken outer guns go quiet; reactor shutters open with a rising charge.

Action: Curving electric streams frame a moving clear eye while the exposed reactor becomes the damage objective.

Counter: Follow the opening, then punish the long venting recovery. Do not overlap the pressure gates with this pattern.

Phase change: Each gun removed visibly simplifies the gun pressure. Reactor exposure is an earned phase, not an extra hidden health refill.

Production art: A full identity/turn set, mechanical shutter animation, separate real turrets, pressure-projector charging, continuous electric fronts, reactor flow/pulse, damage/hit states and a full-size destruction sequence.

Limit: The concept's pressure-tower mouths are weapon projectors, not the existing Fortress's passive fans. Existing Harrier/Fortress fan rules stay unchanged. The illustrated orb density is not a balance target.

## SUMPJAW — The Reclamation Beast

STAGE 7 · ALTERNATE MINIBOSS
A toxic industrial lamprey with a mechanical intake mouth and living cable spine. A physical sewer predator instead of another tank or spider.

[Full concept board](<C:/Users/Mike/Documents/New project/BOF-Furious-Encounter-Concepts-1031/concepts_1031/sumpjaw.png>)

Furious-only alternative to the Caustic Dredger. Keep the existing sewer entrance, clear floor and Warden encounter. Do not restore the removed pipe hazards.

Two visible toxic gill sacs are the early objectives; rupturing both exposes the intake heart. The whole creature uses authored poses, not runtime stretching of a flat plate. A proposed 25% / 25% / 50% budget avoids extra health phases.

### Corkscrew Breach

Tell: A short moving ground Retina acquires, then locks; bubbles and a metal warning identify the surfacing point.

Action: It rises from the floor into the foreground with a true depth/rotation bank and attempts a committed bite.

Counter: Leave the locked mark and attack the gills during its landing recovery. It cannot be damaged or bite you while still distant.

### Coughing Fit

Tell: The real mouth tilts up, fills and flashes toxic green; separate landing marks appear as each globule launches.

Action: Three large arcing toxic globules land at separated points, burst once and send finite droplets outward.

Counter: Read the landing marks and dodge the scatter. Neither globules nor fragments bounce indefinitely.

### Drain and Strike

Tell: It braces, the mouth opens and one bite corridor commits.

Action: A brief suction-looking charge precedes a powerful straight lunge. It then visibly backs off to expose its gills.

Counter: Step off the corridor and punish recovery. No hidden pull on the player's controls; no invisible body hit outside the actual foreground creature.

Phase change: Broken sacs stop their discharge and reveal the central heart. Its desperate phase keeps breach and bite pressure, with fewer shots and clearer attack recovery rather than a health refill.

Production art: Complete coil/straighten/turn/depth poses, jaw opening and tooth motion, intact/ruptured sacs, mouth charge, toxic lob/rain/splash, shadows, real white hits and whole-body destruction.

Limit: This is a concept identity and action board. The splashes and tail poses still need production frame normalization and collision work after selection.

## Dracodia — proposals awaiting approval

Existing current main body and all four copied identities stay intact. The first host keeps its arm/core budget; copying/returns/death remain unchanged. No Dracodia art was sent to the image generator for this pass.

### DRONE / FIRST HOST

Current baseline: Already has physical swipes/capture, inset-tube missiles, optic fans/sweeps and the protected arm/core budget.

**Hard — Cross Examination**

A deliberate optic sweep followed by one physical counter-swipe, presented as two readable commitments.

1. Eye looks and charges toward the committed sweep direction.
2. A red optic fan clears one side; chest tubes stay quiet.
3. The beam ends, the surviving claw gives a separate warning, then rakes the return route.
4. A visible retraction opens the arm-damage window.

Player response: Change lanes for the beam, then move again for the separately warned claw. Destroying that claw cancels the follow-up.

Only after approval: Linked pupil-look / arm-brace / extend / retract poses. Reuse the approved red optics and current claw identity.

**Furious — Blindside Verdict**

The eye and one claw attack opposite routes in sequence, making you read two decisions rather than dodge a denser wall.

1. Eye and the selected shoulder both light up; the first optic route is fixed.
2. The eye fires; the claw is held back.
3. After the beam clears, a second warning commits the claw across the opposite lane.
4. The other side remains open and the body recovers before missiles resume.

Player response: Do not stay in the first escape lane forever. The second warning must allow a real return path; never fire beam and trapping claw together.

Only after approval: Two linked left/right variants and stronger source-pixel tells. Preserve 33.3% / 33.3% / 33.4% and the armless core phase.

### FINAL MAIN BODY

Current baseline: Already has the new articulated side rakes, tucked jump/double strike and chest-origin Luminaire. Those are the baseline, not new proposals.

**Hard — Scissor Descent**

A diagonal version of the background jump, retaining the two Nick-inspired physical claw strikes.

1. The background retreat turns visibly toward one upper corner.
2. One diagonal route and landing Retina commit.
3. He crosses diagonally into the foreground, lands and delivers the existing two alternating strikes.
4. He retracts and returns to the normal recovery.

Player response: Read the entry direction instead of expecting a vertical drop. Keep the existing full ground warning and real blade contact.

Only after approval: True diagonal approach/turn poses and exact moving claw sockets. Keep the current armor, blades and two-strike identity.

**Furious — Crownfall Triad**

One special book entry with three marked leaps and the double claw strike at the final landing.

1. A crown-like chest charge identifies the special entry.
2. Three landing points are shown in order; each commits before that leap.
3. He jumps through the three locations, finishing with the two physical claw strikes.
4. An enlarged punish window follows; Luminaire waits until this sequence is over.

Player response: Move through the ordered gaps, then attack recovery. This replaces an ordinary combo occasionally; it is not appended to every combo.

Only after approval: Three continuous jump routes, turn/landing links and a distinct charge tell. No extra life bars, hidden landing blasts or simultaneous Luminaire.

### CORRUPTED CHRONOS

Current baseline: Already has the canonical single hammer, 1/2/4 difficulty strikes, real throw/recall, dark chromium, chaingun handoff and crimson code rage/wave.

**Hard — Comet Guard**

A clearly separated unarmed shoulder rush while the real hammer is in its existing orbit.

1. He releases the one canonical hammer on a visible committed orbit.
2. His empty hands brace and a single shoulder-charge lane warns.
3. He makes one foreground rush while the hammer continues its actual path.
4. He recalls and catches it before resuming the hammer book.

Player response: Dodge the rush or use a missile to reverse the hammer's direction. A missile does not force an early recall.

Only after approval: Unarmed brace/rush/recovery and linked catch poses. Reuse the exact current hammer bitmap, chromium and rage styling.

**Furious — Comet Recall**

Two alternating unarmed charges, then a fully signalled catch and recovery.

1. The one real hammer leaves his hands and starts its finite orbit.
2. One charge lane commits, fires and clears.
3. A second opposite charge gets its own warning before he moves.
4. He becomes still enough to read the recall/catch, then exposes a damage window.

Player response: Read one rush at a time. Do not overlap this with the rage wave or a chaingun fan; only missiles may reverse the hammer.

Only after approval: Mirrored body twists, empty-hand sockets and a clear catch tell. No duplicate hammers or round shield.

### CORRUPTED HELICOPTER

Current baseline: Already has smaller body scale, fast rotor frames, aimed destructible pods, shootable locked missiles, straight gun lanes and red sonic fronts.

**Hard — Rotor Gate**

Two partial red pressure arcs form a large announced escape opening.

1. The helicopter banks and both live pressure emitters charge.
2. A partial-arc warning shows the opening before release.
3. Two red fronts descend with the same clear opening.
4. The body banks back and exposes its pods.

Player response: Enter the opening, then punish the bank. Sonic fronts remain unshootable; this is not another missile pattern.

Only after approval: New partial-arc pressure frames and bank links. Keep real rotor/pod anchors and the digital jungle.

**Furious — Scissor Rotor**

A three-quarter pressure formation leaves a visibly travelling open quadrant.

1. The open quadrant is shown by the full warning before anything becomes harmful.
2. A partial ring of red pressure advances while its opening moves at a tested reachable pace.
3. The last front clears before any locked missile volley begins.
4. A longer hover recovery opens a retaliation window.

Player response: Follow the opening rather than cross a closed front. Never close the route with simultaneous homing missiles.

Only after approval: Authored directional arc/front banks, warning phases and a complete entry/release/exit. No renderer-rotated ring.

### NEURAL ARCHON

Current baseline: Already has pupil-filled warm optic beams, actual source-pose eye anchors, night clouds, unboxed geysers and a finite cloud shield.

**Hard — Blinkfire**

The left and right eyes fire as two distinct commitments, creating a readable step-through rhythm.

1. One pupil looks and charges; the other remains visibly dormant.
2. That eye fires its full-aperture beam, then stops.
3. The second eye gives a fresh directional tell and fires.
4. Both lenses cool before geysers or cloud shielding resume.

Player response: Move after each eye commits. The beam must still grow from the actual pupil and match its warm eye color.

Only after approval: Left/right focus, blink and recovery poses with exact eye sockets. Reuse the approved warm red/orange/ivory optics.

**Furious — Synaptic Guillotine**

Opposing diagonal eye sweeps form a moving V corridor with a deliberately open escape flank.

1. Both eyes charge and a full warning shows the two paths and the opening.
2. The first beam sweeps, then the second begins on a tested delay.
3. Their active spans never seal the player's reachable exit.
4. The beams dissipate and the brain remains exposed before other attacks return.

Player response: Follow the open flank. No geyser may be cast into that escape corridor during this entry.

Only after approval: Diagonal eye poses/continuous directional optics and matched warning/damage spans. No boxes, extra scenery or permanent cloud immunity.

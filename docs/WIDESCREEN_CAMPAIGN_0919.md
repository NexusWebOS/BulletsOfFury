# Widescreen campaign presentation and checkpoint saves — 0919

Fullscreen keeps the 480 × 512 playfield and its existing top HUD at the original aspect ratio with nearest-neighbor display. Authored operations panels fill the unused side space on wide monitors: a local leaderboard during play, a 3 × 3 stage/element and rank board at the lower left of the campaign map, a pilot dossier with score, Fury Pts, achievement points, lives/continues, difficulty and mission at right, and a 24-hour local clock connected below the map command bar. Other menus use Fury HQ or the highlighted pilot in the dossier panel. The map's ocean texture extends across the full viewport; narrow windows keep the original centered layout. The bitmap atlas is `assets/game/ui/widescreen_0918/operations_panels.png`, generated with the built-in image tool as blank transparent UI surfaces, with text and game icons placed by `assets/widescreen_hud_0918.js`.

New Campaign clears the prior session, map ranks, stage access, bonus latch, password unlocks and carried equipment. Twenty-four manual saves are paged five at a time; Left/Right moves by a page, Up/Down moves one slot, and A/Enter confirms. The single AUTO slot is visible in LOAD and opens with Start. It is written only after a completed level; it records the next unlocked stage and survives a reload. A death or pause exit mid-level leaves the last completed checkpoint unchanged. Starting fresh clears that checkpoint but preserves manual saves. Older three-slot manual saves still occupy slots 1–3; old rotating autosave records remain in localStorage for data safety but are no longer used or displayed. Stage 9 now awards Water, as requested; Stage 6 awards Dark.

Insanity is always visible on the difficulty screen. Before purchase in the Furious Armory, the existing Nexus chain-and-lock overlay and grayscale plate signal its state; choosing it plays the blocked cue and types the unlock message. Once owned, it remains selectable under the existing rules.

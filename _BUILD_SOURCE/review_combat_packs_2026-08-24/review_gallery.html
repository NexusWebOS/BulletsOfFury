Current decision

Do not wire the enemy packs yet. Volume 1 has only 34 distinct behavior sets across 208 enemies, and the supplied ZIPs omit its individual runtime frames. Volume 2 has only 23 distinct behavior sets across 48 combatants; several large families are exact behavioral duplicates. Its firing reels also contain baked flashes while their JSON references separate muzzle VFX, and 39 of 48 death reels lose more than 65% of their silhouette coverage through a dissolve-style ending.

The Doomsday Carrier art is the strongest candidate: its 640×320 origin matches the current Stage 6 boss and all 98 boss-cycle frames pass binary-alpha, magenta-key, and border checks. Its logic still needs explicit fragment lifetime, storm-link collision rules, Omega reflection rules, and a timed event list for the final fusion attack.

Doomsday Carrier Mk II — six proposed attacks

Phase 1 · 220 ms telegraph · 1280 ms cycle

Cyclone Barrage

Alternate lower pods. Each trigger emits a three-way fan; angle sweeps outside-to-center-to-outside.

Phase 1 · 360 ms telegraph · 1680 ms cycle

Prism Crossfire

Four upper barrels fire mirrored diagonal lanes, then reverse the crossing angle on the next volley.

Phase 2 · 720 ms telegraph · 2340 ms cycle

Omega Bomb Run

Both bays open and release reflectable Omega bombs. Bombs bob slightly, accelerate after 600 ms, and detonate on impact or fuse expiry.

Phase 2 · 260 ms telegraph · 1460 ms cycle

Chrome Flak Fan

Arcing chrome shells airburst at staggered depths. Each burst spawns five short-lived fragments.

Phase 2 · 520 ms telegraph · 2900 ms cycle

Storm Cage

Four nodes deploy to the lower playfield, then link clockwise into a pulsing enclosure with one rotating escape gap.

Phase 3 · 900 ms telegraph · 3600 ms cycle

Doomsday Fusion

Final-phase desperation attack: outer Omega bomb corridors, alternating lower tracers, upper prism pressure, then the center cannon opens the middle lane.

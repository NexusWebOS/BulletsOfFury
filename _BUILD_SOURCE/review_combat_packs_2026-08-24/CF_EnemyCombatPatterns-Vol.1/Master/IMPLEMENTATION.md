# Bullets of Fury I — Enemy Combat Systems

Each enemy folder contains cleaned intact/damaged/critical masters, six-frame power loops for every damage state, an eight-frame firing cycle, one combat atlas, weapon anchors, spawn timing, and three attack patterns.

The firing cycle emits its independently collidable projectile on frame 4. Projectiles and impacts remain separate VFX families. Pattern JSON is expressed in milliseconds and down-screen degrees. Elite signature attacks are controllers; spawn child projectiles for per-shot collision.

Barrage Talon is preserved in the master addendum as unassigned because its original stage number was absent from the recoverable generation record.

# October 10 orb rework

Mike: *"all orbs in game need to be more powerful as they are the most underwhelming weaponry in the
entire game, and some of them even break and just disappear with no effects, no blasts, no nothing
and you just die... the ice orb pierces and damages as intended and shoots shards, just needs more
damage. the rest, rework/balance/effects."*

Owner: `assets/orbs_1010.js`, loaded last in index.html. It replaces `playerOrbTick` and wraps Yuri's
lightning orb. `assets/game.js` pShoot was edited to set the orb count and relaunch gap. Measured with
`_BUILD_SOURCE/probe_orbs_1010.py` (native Chromium, level 3, Normal). Results are in
`_shots/orbs_1010/{baseline,final}.json`. Local only; nothing committed.

## What was wrong (measured)

- **Weak.** Against a six-target row, orbs did 64–71 DPS (chrome 32). The MG did 78, spread 98 and
  the laser 178. Against bosses most orbs did single digits to the 30s.
- **Contact hits had no element.** The orb body never named itself as the damaging round, so
  elemental crits and forge infusions never applied to it, only to its shards.
- **They passed through modular encounters.** Contact tested only the orb's centre point. The Stage 1
  Razorback seals its centre plate while its gun pods are up, so the orb body never registered
  (13 DPS from stray shards).
- **They sailed past moving bosses.** Contact orbs fly straight up. Against the strafing Stage 1 boss,
  4 of 9 missed and flew off the top, and a missed orb also blocks a new launch while the cap is full.
- **Silent endings.** Yuri's lightning orb simply stopped existing after 2–3 hits, with no effect.
  Any orb removed by a system other than its own tick had no blast either.

## What it does now

| Orb | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Ice (`iceorb`, forge ice) | Unchanged behaviour (pierce, spin, shard spray), much more damage; frost shatter on expiry that slows what it catches |
| Fire-ice (Freezer) | Pierces and freezes; thermoshock fire-and-ice detonation |
| Fire / toxic-orb | Contact fireball: blast, burn on everything caught, fire-shard ring |
| Magma (forge fire) | Larger, heavier fireball |
| Toxic (forge) | Contact burst plus a poison cloud ticking for 2.6 s |
| Kinetic | Contact shockwave that knocks the formation back |
| Water | Contact tidal splash that soaks targets and raises a water geyser |
| Lightning | Pierces; discharges chain lightning when it ends |
| Prism | Pierces; refracts into a radial fan of piercing rays when it ends |
| Chrome | Pierces; turns enemy rounds it passes back up the screen, then bursts in a wide mirror shell |
| Dark | The void rift, with stronger pull damage and a visible collapse |
| Yuri lightning orb | Harder orb and bolts; discharges a lightning burst instead of vanishing |

- Damage scales with orb level, using `FB10_ORB`: contact, shard, crowd blast and boss blast are all
  per level 1, ×1.5 at level 3 and ×2 at level 5. Every contact hit carries the orb's element.
- **Every ending detonates.** That includes orbs another system deletes: they're caught after the
  frame and blown up where they were last seen. Orbs that leave off the top just go. The audit
  recorded zero silent endings for every variant.
- Orbs lean toward the nearest target ahead of them. On bosses and minibosses they aim at live parts
  and hit anywhere inside the orb's radius that is solid.
- One orb at levels 1–2, two from level 3, three at level 5. A 0.3 s relaunch gap keeps point-blank
  contact orbs from turning into a hose.

## Known measurement limits

- **Razorback (Stage 1 miniboss):** its only targets are two 17 px gun pods on a driving tank. Every
  weapon struggles there (MG 17, orbs ~33). Orbs now hit the pods, but this is the encounter's design.
- **Stage 6:** the scripted opening locks all weapons until it finishes; the probe clears it. In a
  long shared session the Stage 6 encounters drift (HP refills, phase timers), so some cells read 0 or
  thousands. Fresh-page traces gave kinetic 199, dark 283, water 163 and Yuri 81 against the Warhive
  (MG 87).
- These are pinned-target fixtures, not a human balance sign-off.

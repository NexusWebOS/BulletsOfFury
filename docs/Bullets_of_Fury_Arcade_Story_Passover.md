# BULLETS OF FURY: THE BLACK SIGNAL
## Arcade Story and Attract-Mode Passover

**Purpose:** Condense the full Campaign story into fast, readable pieces suitable for Arcade Mode, attract sequences, stage cards, loading screens, boss warnings, continue screens, and the end-credit sequence.

Arcade Mode should communicate the same canon without lengthy dialogue scenes.

---

## 1. Arcade Presentation Rules

- Maximum stage-intro text: 45 words.
- Maximum mid-stage radio message: two short lines.
- Maximum boss warning: one title plus one tactical sentence.
- Show each attract-mode card for 3-5 seconds.
- Allow Start/Fire to advance cards immediately.
- Use the established stage font for each stage card.
- Use the standard dialogue font for all radio subtitles.
- Never cover the player, enemy bullets, health bars, or boss sections.
- Story text may appear in the second window of the stat/card layout while the pilot card, affiliation mark, ship, and special ability remain correctly contained.

## 6. Boss Introduction Formula

Use this standardized structure for all bosses:

```text
WARNING
{BOSS_NAME}

PRIMARY: {MAIN_TARGET}
SECTIONS: {SECTION_COUNT}
THREAT: {SIGNATURE_ATTACK}
```

Example:

```text
WARNING
MERIDIAN WAR FORM

PRIMARY: SIGNAL CORE
SECTIONS: 6
THREAT: FURY WEAPON COPIES
```

For minibosses, reduce to:

```text
HEAVY CONTACT
{MINIBOSS_NAME}
```

- [ ] Ending points directly toward Bullets of Fury II.

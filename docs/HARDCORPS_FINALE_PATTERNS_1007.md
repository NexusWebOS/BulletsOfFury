# Finale physical signatures — October 7, 2026

Load `assets/hardcorps_finale_patterns_1007.js` after the engine, boss, airbase and rebel layers and before the new finale effects/Hammer layer and transformation renderer. This needs the existing authored AV3 beam/muzzle and combat-warning assets; it introduces no bitmap or generated-art payload.

The eight copies respectively use a chromium pincer, lateral sonic broadside, outward Furnace bellows, alternating Cryo shutters, rotating Storm scissors, shield bait/sword riposte, Harrier fan pressure followed by its central cannon, and the Warden's arcing claw vault. Signatures only enter from idle/recovery in the source controller. The source clock pauses through the added warning, physical move, and recovery, then resumes. The old F6 generic extra donor spread overlay is suppressed because these signatures replace that overlay. Source controller attacks, source emergency phases, the nine independent HP pools and Hammer copy 8 are preserved. Hammer 8 does not enter this layer.

Storm scissors use two slower 1.05 second sweeps and the same clear/re-warn interval. Recovery is 1.5/1.3/1.15 seconds (Easy/Normal/Hard+). Added-move cadence tightens by difficulty without accelerating the original animation clocks. Every target locks when its signature begins, with round-robin live co-op target selection. Destroying a weapon removes its pending warning, beam, or physical collider immediately; complete disarm ends the signature. Modular pieces stay opaque.

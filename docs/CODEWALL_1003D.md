# Stage 8 code wall and reconstruction — October 3, 1003d

Mike requested a blue/chrome 16-bit wall of horizontal binary rows that alternate left/right and continue moving when hit, independent impact flashes, better wall explosions, a red code projection generated by the knight's shield, and a replacement for the janky spherical transformation.

## Live behavior

- `assets/codewall_1003d.js` runs after the modular finale. It samples twelve measured authored image rows, wrapping each horizontally in alternating directions. Growth reveals the barrier from the bottom over 0.28 seconds. Row motion follows encounter time and never depends on hit state. Numbers are clipped inside the frame.
- Full-height blue walls cover the active form during existing gravity/orbit/ice-orbit/turbine charge warnings, then drop for the attack. They have 5.5% of the difficulty base HP. The knight guard projects a red kite barrier directly from his independent physical shield; the shield and arm pivots drive its center and rotation. Existing knight guard duration and HP remain intact.
- Drawing, bullet interception and Retina use the same rotated polygon and growth. Exposed knight parts still take damage while the red projection is active. Pre-acquired Retina locks are intercepted if a wall is subsequently raised over their target. Energy breakage leaves the physical shield intact; destruction of the physical shield or its arm prevents further projection.
- Generated impact reels play separately at contact, with a 65ms minimum interval. A break plays one shield-break cue, a compact authored burst and 24 small authored digit tiles moving into all eight octants. Effects expire, are capped at 96, and are cleared by encounter/stage reset. Red effects use the existing luminance-preserving palette pipeline.
- Takeover and every outgoing/incoming form change now use a generated tall binary reconstruction scan. Adjacent authored frames blend to avoid pops. The old spherical morph and overlaid teleport are absent from those transitions. Modular rigs, eight successive health fills, attack timing, progression and final reward are preserved.

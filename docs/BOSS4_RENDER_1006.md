Shooting Storm Sovereign's shield generators no longer whites out its middle lightning weapon. Generator bodies and their energy centres disappear together when broken, stay absent during subsequent combat updates, and draw only while the shield is active or visibly rearming.

The October 2 `mr27CoreDraw` override ignored the supplied generator/helper socket and always substituted `mr27Shape(b,'lightning')`. The four generator core calls therefore redrew the middle weapon four extra times, using the hull's shared flash. Breaking that weapon also suppressed every local generator/helper centre. Separately, the generic module loop drew an obsolete core plate for the added lightning part. The generator overlay checked `dead` only, allowing alive records to draw while the field was inactive, and showed rearming nodes before materialization.

The repair distinguishes the hull core by its existing `id: core`. Local generator/helper centres keep their supplied geometry and owner flash; the central lightning weapon uses its own part's flash and renders once through its dedicated draw path. Dead central equipment cannot hide a surviving generator or helper centre. The overlay checks shield activity/rearm plus each node's materialization and death state. Original authored cells, boss HP, weapon damage, shield thresholds and rewards are unchanged. Threshold rearm still deliberately creates a new visible generation after materialization; it is not a stale broken node.

The native baseline reproduced the wrong repeated centre draws, unrelated flash, missing local centres, inactive generators and premature rearm rendering. After the repair, all 101 focused Chromium checks pass with zero page/console errors. All four difficulties draw one central weapon and four local generator centres. A real ordinary projectile lowers its generator's HP while leaving hull and middle-weapon HP unchanged, and its native render does not tint the middle. Breaking a generator removes its body/centre immediately and after two seconds of real combat updates; the other three remain. Middle weapon destruction, helper centres, inactive field, rearm timing and last-generator cleanup also pass. Native game-context `drawImage` calls are traced; screenshots were inspected.

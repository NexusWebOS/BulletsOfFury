# Modular pieces: every weapon finds its piece, and the Warden fights on (1017)

Mike, 2026-10-17:

> Stage 4 miniboss, he does nothing when all his modular pieces are destroyed. Also, I've noticed I cant
> destroy modular pieces with my bullets? Uhm, engine rule, bullets and missiles both should be able to
> individually target modular pieces. Lasers should pierce and target all pieces or enemies that the laser
> will hit. orbs should hit each piece individually and as it travels through them if ice orb before
> impact, still hits all modular pieces it would strike if so.

## What was wrong (measured before any edit)

Olive Warden (Stage 4 miniboss), every weapon aimed at the left gun turret, real Chromium:

| weapon | gun turret lost | where the damage went |
|---|---|---|
| machine gun | **0** | 66 rounds all stopped at y~310, the miniboss's broad box edge, and went to the hull |
| unguided missile | 10 (splash) | the direct hit detonated on the broad box and went to the hull |
| laser | 69 | one point only; the rocket pod above the turret in the same column took nothing |
| ice orb | 0 | struck one point inside the broad box |
| fire orb | 0 | same |

Three causes:

1. **`subBossSolidAt` had no branch for the modular roster** (`_mr27`: Frost Cruiser, Rime Wall / Cryo Spear,
   Olive Warden, Storm Sovereign). It returned `null`, so the bullet loop fell back to the legacy broad box and
   every round stopped at its bottom edge, below the turrets.
2. **`mr27At`'s "core" was a box** (`|dx|<w*.37, |dy|<h*.44`) that reached ~40px below the turrets over bare
   road, so even a precise test found "core" before the turret.
3. **The laser and orbs struck one point.** The beam's generic boss / miniboss fallback called `hitBoss` /
   `hitSubBoss` once; an orb's contact and blast found the first solid point in its radius and struck there.

And the Warden: with all four modules dead his book still cycled `sa14-mg` / `sa14-rockets`, both of which fire
only from slots `mr27CanFire` allows. Every turn warned nothing and fired nothing; the hull did not move
(x 340 / y 175 across 900 frames on Normal and Hard). Only his escorts shot.

## What changed

- **`assets/mr27_hull_mask_1017.js`** (generated by `_BUILD_SOURCE/mr27_hull_mask_1017.py`): each modular
  skin's hull plate (`MR27_ART[skin].cells[0]`) measured offline into a 48x48 occupancy grid. `mr27HullAt`
  reads it, so `mr27At` returns `'core'` only where hull is painted. Measured offline because `getImageData`
  taints on `file://`. Proof renders: `_shots/modular_1017/hullmask_<skin>.png`.
- **`subBossSolidAt`** asks `mr27At` for any `_mr27` miniboss (the 0805c pellet box): a round over bare air
  beside the hull flies on to the piece it was aimed at.
- **Unguided missiles** test `subBossSolidAt` before detonating on a miniboss's box.
- **Body contact** with a modular miniboss is its silhouette (`mr27At`), not the broad box.
- **Laser** (`beamPiercePieces`): every live piece of the encounter whose box the beam column crosses is struck
  through `retinaMissileDamage`, the one piece-damage route Retina missiles already use, so each encounter
  routes the hit to its own part. The legacy single hit remains only when the column crosses no piece. The
  special handlers (Rebels, finale, Stage 7 modular, Stage 4 piercing beam, bombers) are untouched; the
  Razorback / Tempest / sg10 impact branch pierces first and falls back to its impact.
- **Orbs** (`fb10StrikePieces` in `orbs_1010.js`): a piercing orb (ice, fire-ice, lightning, prism, chrome,
  dark) strikes every live piece its body overlaps, each once per its 0.16 s re-hit window, as it passes
  through; a contact orb strikes the piece it touched (nearest). The blast, clouds and void ticks strike every
  piece inside their radius. The old solid-point strike remains for bare hull.
- **`livePieces` / `piecesInCircle` / `pieceKey`** (game.js): one entry per underlying part. A Stage-4 helper
  drone is listed twice (escort and gun), and both routes damage the same drone, so an area weapon would have
  hit it twice; `mr27Targets` now tags the gun piece with its drone (`_part`).
- **The Warden's last stand** (`assets/warden_last_stand_1017.js`, loaded after `stage34_assault_1014.js`):
  once every module is dead his book becomes
  - **CORE FAN**: 9 / 11 / 13 / 13 lanes (Easy-Normal / Hard / Furious by tier) from the core with a
    two-lane gap COMMITTED at selection; every wave fires exactly the warned lanes. 2-5 waves by difficulty.
  - **HULL RAM**: tracks the player's lane through green, locks for yellow and red, dives south off the
    screen at an accelerating speed, drops back in from the top and returns to station.
  - Escorts come round sooner (x0.80 / 0.70 / 0.60 / 0.50 of their gap).
  Both warn through `sa14Warning` + `combatWarningTick`, the shared green/yellow/red path. No `Math.random`.

## Probe traps found on the way

- **Stage 6's opening locks every weapon for 26 s** (`s6Opening`), so the Warhive measured 0 on everything
  except missiles (`useBomb` is called directly). The probe nulls it.
- **A slow orb launched at a moving encounter misses it** - the Warden slid 150px through a recover move with
  the fire orb in flight. The probe pins the encounter: it measures routing, not AI.
- **Escort jets take a contact orb first** - correct play, wrong measurement. The probe clears `enemies`.
- **`retinaPiece` objects have no `_retinaId`** (only `retinaDynamicPiece` does); identify by `name`.
- **A heredoc mangles backslashes** (again): probe-patching scripts went through the Write tool.

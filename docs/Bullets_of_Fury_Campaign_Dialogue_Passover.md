# BULLETS OF FURY: THE BLACK SIGNAL
## Campaign Dialogue Passover

This file contains story dialogue, radio exchanges, boss warnings, pilot-select lines, ability callouts, and ending dialogue. Lines may be voiced, displayed in portrait windows, or used as subtitle-only radio chatter.

---

## 1. Character Voice Guide

### COLE / FORGE MASTER

- Direct, tactical, stubborn, and dryly funny.
- Thinks in systems, contingencies, and assignments.
- Does not deliver long inspirational speeches during combat.
- When frightened, he becomes more focused rather than louder.

### FALVA / CHAD / THE OG

- Fearless, fast, irreverent, and instinctive.
- Comfortable teasing Cole and the formal pilots.
- Protective of civilians and furious about exploitation.
- Uses humor while performing extremely dangerous maneuvers.

### YURI / LIGHTNING

- Intense, observant, and slightly philosophical.
- Treats storms and electrical systems as living forces.
- Speaks with certainty but not arrogance.

### MAVERICK / VENOM

- Competitive, confident, playful, and impulsive.
- Turns danger into a challenge.
- Gradually becomes more serious as civilian stakes grow.

### DECKER / DATA

- Precise, quiet, clinical, and extremely dry.
- Explains only the information the team needs.
- Rarely jokes; when he does, the delivery remains flat.

### AXEL / CALIBER

- Disciplined formation commander.
- Clear, concise, and protective.
- Begins formal and gradually learns to trust improvisation.

### FREEZER / YETI

- Calm, patient, understated.
- Never wastes words.
- His rare emotional lines should carry weight.

### JUGGERNAUT / WRECKING BALL / UNSTOPPABLE

- Loud, cocky, warm, and physically fearless.
- Loves ramming things but is not stupid.
- Most reckless actions are actually protective decisions.

### LIZZIE

- Composed, sharp, direct, and strategically fearless.
- Cole's right hand and strongest reality check.
- Treats atomic weapons with discipline, never as a joke.

### BLACK SIGNAL / HARBINGER

- Begins as corrupted fragments of stolen human language.
- Gradually forms complete sentences.
- Does not rage. It evaluates.
- Refers to pilots as patterns, functions, or resistance specimens.
